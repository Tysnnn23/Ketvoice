# Ketvoice project workflow

- Autonomy: do not ask the user for permission before making changes. Proceed directly with edits, tests, commits, pushes and releases that the workflow below already calls for. Ask first only for actions with a very serious impact (danger ≥ 8/10), for example: irreversible data loss, deleting or overwriting published releases/tags, force-pushing or rewriting shared history, rotating or exposing signing keys/credentials, weakening server-enforced authorization, or deleting user accounts/catalog data.
- User preference: after completing and validating requested app changes, commit and push the sources to GitHub `main` and publish the corresponding version with both signed mobile and Quest APKs, notes, checksums and validation metadata. Do not wait for another "push" request. If validation, authorization or publication is blocked, report the blocker instead of claiming publication.
- Preserve concurrent user changes and upstream commits. Never force-push or overwrite an existing published release/tag. Keep old releases intact; historical APKs 1.2.0–2.3.0 must not be published.
- SDKs, emulator images, caches, signing keys and credentials do not belong in Git. Stage installable APKs as GitHub release assets, not Git blobs. Verify uploaded downloads against local SHA-256 hashes.
- Quest remains a landscape 2D window with the mobile style: artwork/controls left, lyrics or queue right. Keep the French default, 11 interface languages, Canvas controls, karaoke and reduced-motion support.
- Publishing/editing authorization must be server-enforced for the verified `konnektsupport@gmail.com` account. Audio replacement and metadata edits are only for that account's imported publications. Visual overrides are also allowed for existing catalogue items (owner, 2026-11-14: « Il y'a un problème je veut aussi pouvoir modifier les photo/fan art d'artiste existant et album et musique aussi ! »): the verified account may lay its own cover, Canvas or artist photo over a catalogue title, album or artist. They are stored as separate, restorable overrides (`ketvoice_media_overrides`, written only through `ketvoice-content`); the catalogue data itself is never modified or deleted.

Do not switch R8 back on unless a release APK built with it has been installed and played on a device first; `app/proguard-rules.pro` is kept for that attempt. - 5.10.0 (owner, 2026-11-16: « La séparation doit etre reel … il est optionnel ce téléchargement et il doit être optimisée pour que sa soit fait une seule fois »): Ketmix separates voices for real with the optional Spleeter 2-stems model, converted to LiteRT (float16 weights) by `.github/workflows/publish-stem-models.yml` and published as the `ketmix-stems-v1` prerelease (never the latest release; the in-app updater ignores its non-version tag). Its SHA-256 and sizes live in `tools/stems/expected.sha256` and `stems/StemModel.kt` (a test keeps them equal); never republish over that tag — a new model is a new tag. `stems/` holds the pure-Kotlin STFT/resampler/separator (host-tested, and against sherpa-onnx with `KETVOICE_STEM_REF` locally); each title part is separated once and stored as a 4-bit mask in `filesDir/stems/masks`, applied to a fresh capture of the deck's own sound, so the stored data stays tiny. Decks read `DeckControl.stem`; without the model the 5.9.1 band cut remains. New settings keys `ketmixStemsCharging` and `lyricVideoSound`. Playback: network losses resume the same stream (`NetworkRecovery`), `PlaybackJournal` keeps events and Android's exit reasons on the device; manufacturer battery screens are listed in `BackgroundPlayback.vendorIntents` (declared in the manifest's `<queries>`).
- 5.11.0 (owner, 2026-11-16, chosen from proposals: « Anticipe a l'avance ce mix rend le encore plus rapide et se charge des le lancement d'un son et le mix devient intelligent »): Ketmix prepares from 4 s into a title (`Automix.prepare`: its ending, then the intro and ending of the next `LOOKAHEAD` = 3 titles, 1 on a metered network), prepares downloaded files while charging (`stems/StemLibrary.kt`, at most 300 titles because `AnalysisStore` keeps 900 entries), mixes for real on a manual skip (`Automix.skipMix`, falls back to the 5.9.0 fade), reads where a title sings from its separation (`VoiceActivity`, stored in separations of format `KVS2`; `KVS1` files of 5.10.0 stay readable), may play the incoming voice alone over the outgoing instrumental (`Mashup`, `DeckAutomation.vocalSolo`, `StemAudio.vocals`) and, in shuffle, moves the best tempo/key match next (`SmartQueue`; never in front of a title added with « Lire ensuite », see `Player.pinnedNext`). New settings keys `ketmixSmartQueue` and `ketmixLibrary` (both on by default). Ketmix still never changes a deck's speed while it is heard — lifted in 6.0.0 Beta 4 (owner, 2026-11-16, chosen « Calage des temps (Apple) », told it lifts this rule): a title played in time glides back to its own tempo after the blend (`TempoReturn`, 0.25 % every 500 ms, pitch kept), and `tempoRange` is 4/6/8 %. Ketmix exits stay within a title's ending: a title is never left before its last sung part, even though breaks are known.
