Compose Multiplatform, Kotlin and AndroidX (Ketvoice for Windows)

Ketvoice for Windows draws its window with Compose Multiplatform 1.12.2 (org.jetbrains.compose,
with Skiko, Material 3 and the AndroidX libraries it brings, such as navigation-compose 2.10.0 and
lifecycle) and runs on the Kotlin 2.2.21 standard library and kotlinx.coroutines 1.11.0,
