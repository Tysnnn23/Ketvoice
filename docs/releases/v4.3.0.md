# Ketvoice 4.3.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

## Navigation that keeps its place

- The active glass lens slides, stretches and settles with a controlled spring.
  Icons transition in size, opacity and lift; label weight and colour change
  smoothly in the expanded capsule. The compact capsule retains its moving lens.
- Root-tab content moves in the direction of selection. Saved tab state includes
  list/grid scroll positions, rather than resetting the page on every switch.
- Artist and album pages reveal from the selected card's measured window bounds.
  Closing reverses toward that stored origin, including in adaptive-width pages.
  Both pages retain their own request while overlapping: fast artist-to-artist
  navigation no longer substitutes new data into the outgoing page.
- The mini-player remains mounted independently of detail navigation. Reduced
  motion removes the spatial transitions and staggered entrances.

## Artist and album pages

- Artist profiles have a quiet loading skeleton, image-derived accents,
  progressively revealed artwork, staggered identity/actions and scroll-driven
  hero depth. The floating title and scrim appear continuously as the hero moves
  away. Scroll-driven layer properties avoid whole-header recomposition per pixel.
- Album artwork still starts behind the status bar, edge to edge. It reveals
  progressively and its pixels dissolve vertically into an album-coloured surface;
  no broad white overlay bleaches the image. The local tint then meets the page.
- Album metadata/actions enter together, loading track rows use discreet skeletons,
  and list updates animate in place. The top-bar title follows actual scroll bounds
  instead of switching abruptly when the first list item disappears.
- Canvas controls, independent album/player Canvas settings, queue swipes and the
  landscape 2D Quest layout remain. French remains the default among 11 languages.

## Bit-Perfect, with a real contract and an honest fallback

- **Settings → Playback → Bit-Perfect** requests Android 14+'s public
  `AudioMixerAttributes.MIXER_BEHAVIOR_BIT_PERFECT` contract. An advertised compatible
  mixer is required. Android currently exposes this path for compatible USB outputs;
  ordinary wired, Bluetooth and speaker paths normally remain standard output.
- A separate native `AudioTrack` writes decoded PCM without the standard sink's
  PCM conversion or app processing. Sample rate, encoding/bit depth and channel
  configuration must match the advertised mixer exactly. The app follows the real
  system route; an idle USB device does not steal playback from Bluetooth.
- **Active** is never inferred from the switch, audio focus or capability alone.
  It requires an accepted and still-owned mixer preference, the actual routed
  device, matching AudioTrack PCM, real bytes written while playing, unity app gain
  and no app DSP. Pause, seek/reset, loss of route or ownership revoke that status.
- Unsupported formats/outputs, refused preferences, digital attenuation and
  unverifiable routes fall back to standard playback with a reason. A removed
  USB/wired output pauses playback rather than unexpectedly playing on the speaker.
- EQ, reverb, skip-silence, Sonic Canvas PCM tapping and progressive quality handoff
  are bypassed while requested. Their settings remain available for standard mode;
  turning the mode off restores the ordinary processing path. AutoMix, normalization
  and crossfade are not added by this release.
- The settings status panel shows decoded PCM, negotiated output and device only
  when known. The player's audio readout remains **Appearance → Stats info**, optional
  and off by default, with no permanent audio badge.
- This is **not a proprietary exclusive USB driver**, a promise that every DAC
  supports exact playback, or proof of the original studio master/analog output.
  A lossy source remains lossy. System/hardware volume is still Android's concern;
  the app never claims to bypass vendor hardware or OS limits. The Active label
  describes the Android mixer contract for decoded PCM, not a physical bit capture.

## Validation and remaining limits

- Mobile and Quest JVM tests cover the proof table, PCM format mismatches, saved
  navigation state, nested back origins, outgoing route identity, rapid reversal,
  reduced motion, loading states and album scrolling. Native software host previews
  are not device screenshots.
- Robolectric platform fixtures exercise native sink writes, pause/seek/reconfigure,
  mixer refusal/ownership loss, route loss, incompatible rate/depth, Bluetooth/
  speaker refusal and non-unity-gain fallback. **These are mocked Android contracts,
  not real USB DAC playback or hardware bit comparisons.**
- Signed Mobile and Quest APKs, lint results, checksums and exact test counts are
  recorded in the release's `validation.json`. Instrumentation APKs are compiled,
  not run. Uploaded public downloads are checked against local SHA-256 hashes.
- No phone, emulator or physical Quest was connected. Real USB/wired DAC negotiation,
  disconnect behaviour, hardware blur/Canvas, gesture feel, 60/90/120 FPS, live audio
  providers/downloads and Discord account consent/presence remain unvalidated.
- In-process download pause still does not promise recovery after force-stop/reboot.
  Group listening remains deferred; publishing authorization is unchanged.

Android contract references: [preferred mixer attributes](https://source.android.com/docs/core/audio/preferred-mixer-attr)
and [AudioMixerAttributes](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/AudioMixerAttributes).
