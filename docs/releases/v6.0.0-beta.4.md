# Ketvoice 6.0.0 Beta 4

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketmix mixes like Apple Music's AutoMix, the voice slider answers at once, a new glass icon, and a finer Ket 27.

## Ketmix

- **In time, then back home**: songs whose tempos are up to 6 % apart (8 % on the DJ setting) are now blended on the beat. The incoming song plays the blend at the outgoing tempo, then glides back to its own tempo in steps too small to hear, its pitch untouched.
- **A three-band mixer**: besides the bass that changes hands on the beat, the mids of only one song are full at a time during a blend in time, and they step back further when the two keys clash.
- **Harmonic and phrased**: keys that clash are never left to rub for long, and crossfades start where a phrase of the outgoing song starts.

## The voice of a song

- **Ready before you touch it**: the song is separated as soon as its lyrics are shown, so the voice slider answers at once; the high-quality model then improves it in the background.
- **No reload**: moving the slider, or a separation arriving while the song plays, changes the voice live, without a gap or a click.
- **Cleaner**: the "s" and "t" of the voice go with it now, above 11 kHz too; Ketmix's instrumentals benefit as well.
- The lyrics follow the sound exactly while the voice is adjusted.

## Faster, better quality

- **Progressive quality that really switches**: playback starts on the first stream found, then moves to the best one (up to Hi-Res) without a gap: the two streams are aligned on their sound itself and crossfaded. On by default.

## Ket 27

- **New app icon**: the glass K in four versions (Liquid Glass, Light, Tinted, Clear); choose it in Settings with a preview of each.
- **Glass**: a transparency slider like iOS 27's, from very clear to tinted; reflections that follow how you hold the phone; the cover's colours glide across pages, buttons and glass, always readable.
- **Player**: the artwork or Canvas fills the screen behind controls in glass.
- **Lyrics that glow**: a living background in the cover's colours, and each word lights up as it is sung.
- **Home** with large mix cards, **Library and Downloads** with glass filters and cover grids.
- **Search** floats in a glass bubble at the bottom; **covers zoom** into their page and back; an optional **tab bar that shrinks** as you scroll (Settings → Appearance).

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.0.0-beta.4.apk` (phone) or `Ketvoice-6.0.0-beta.4-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
