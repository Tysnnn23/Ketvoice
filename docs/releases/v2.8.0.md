# Ketvoice 2.8.0 — glass, motion, equalizer & local Wi-Fi handoff

**Mobile/tablet and Quest — versionCode 17 — 23 October 2026**

Download **`Ketvoice-2.8.0.apk`** for Android 12+ phones and tablets, and **`Ketvoice-2.8.0-Quest.apk`** for Quest 3/3S. Both are built from the same source and keep the existing interface: no new dashboard, no oversized Quest toolbar.

## Liquid Glass, refined

- Tighter bevel and refraction on glass panels, a soft gradient rim, and a slightly lighter tint. The result stays readable on artwork and Canvas without turning into an opaque card.
- Buttons, transport controls, sliders, and sheets share the same surface treatment.
- On Quest, hovering a control now highlights it with light that follows the pointer. No hand-tracking permission and no 3D scene are added.

## Motion

- One timing vocabulary: 220 ms fades, 140 ms presses, 380 ms lyric follow. Decorative motion no longer uses unrelated spring and duration values.
- A new **Animations réduites** setting, and Android's system animator scale, remove decoration while keeping lyric timing, seeking, and karaoke accurate.
- The three intro dots now match the requested Apple Music behaviour: **three fixed-size dots that light up one after another**. No halo, no growing circle, no merge and no zoom. The dots keep a reserved slot, so the first lyric does not jump when they disappear.
- Lyrics stay visible during the intro, follow the active line, and stop following while you scroll manually. Seeking re-enables following.
- Karaoke text masks are built once per line layout instead of once per playback frame, and Canvas video pauses when the app is not visible.
- Queue rows keep stable per-occurrence keys, so duplicate songs, insertions, and reordering no longer rebuild the wrong row.

## Player polish

- Wide layouts align title, artist, seek, transport, and volume on the same left edge as the artwork.
- The favorite star animates its colour and gives a short press feedback; touch targets are at least 48 dp while the visible icons keep their previous size.
- **Canvas panoramique** is a new optional setting, **off by default**. When enabled, the Canvas plays as a dimmed, darkened background and the foreground artwork stays static to avoid a second video decoder.

## New: native equalizer

- Five bands (60 Hz, 230 Hz, 910 Hz, 3.6 kHz, 14 kHz) with presets (Flat, Bass, Voice, Bright, Rock) and ±12 dB range in the interface.
- The effect runs on the real ExoPlayer audio session through Android's native `Equalizer`. Gains are mapped to the bands and millibel range the device actually exposes; unsupported ranges are clamped instead of pretending to apply.
- If the device has no equalizer, the screen says so. Settings persist, and the effect is released when the playback service is destroyed or its audio session changes.
- Verified on the emulator: the effect chain appears in `dumpsys media.audio_flinger` for the playback session.

## New: same-Wi-Fi handoff, no QR code and no account

- Enable **Transfert sur le même Wi-Fi** on both devices. Ketvoice advertises itself with mDNS and lists nearby Ketvoice instances on the same private IPv4 subnet.
- Choose the recipient. The receiver must **explicitly accept** the request before anything plays. A device on the same Wi-Fi cannot take over playback silently.
- The current track, playback position, shuffle/repeat/autoplay flags and up to 50 queue entries are sent. Only catalog metadata crosses the network: **no cookies, account tokens, stream URLs, local files, or source configuration**.
- The receiver resolves the catalog itself. Messages are bounded, unknown fields are rejected, nonces are single-use, and the start command works once, after approval.
- The sender pauses only after the receiver confirms it is ready. A refusal, timeout, or failure keeps local playback untouched.
- Works only with both apps in the foreground on the same private network. Guest Wi-Fi or access-point isolation blocks discovery, and the screen says so.

## Also

- Lyrics text size (Standard, Large, Very large) now applies to phones and tablets too, not only Quest.
- The publisher music-upload fix from 2.7.0 is unchanged; no backend change is made in this release.

## Limits

- **No physical Quest 3/3S was available.** Horizon OS pointer behaviour, window resizing, audio mixing, thermals, and frame pacing still need headset testing. No Meta Store certification or fixed-FPS guarantee is claimed.
- Quest remains a **2D app window**, landscape-only, not an immersive VR environment.
- The equalizer depends on the device's native audio effect support; some devices expose fewer bands or a narrower gain range.
- The Wi-Fi handoff requires a private IPv4 network without client isolation, both apps open, and tracks available in the online catalog. Transfers of local imports or custom-source tracks are intentionally refused.
- Discovery and receiver consent/refusal were checked on Android; the complete protocol was tested on a JVM loopback socket. **A full playback handoff between two physical devices has not been verified.**
- LAN messages use unencrypted HTTP, not authenticated pairing. Device names are self-reported, not verified identities. Enable this option only on a trusted private Wi-Fi network; other network participants may observe catalog metadata and position. No end-to-end encryption is claimed.
- No real mailbox-owner login or real music upload was performed for this release. External services, email limits, and storage quotas are unchanged.
- Previous releases (2.7.1, 2.7.0, 2.6.1, 2.6.0, 2.5.0) are preserved. Historical 1.2.0–2.3.0 APKs remain withheld.
