# Ketvoice for Windows 1.1.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Windows 11 glass, a full-screen player, buttons in the taskbar preview, Ketmix Studio on the computer, voices ready before you play, and a lighter, smoother app.

## Look

- **Windows 11 glass**: the window takes the Mica material of Windows 11, and the sidebar and the player bar become frosted glass above it. On Windows 10, with transparency effects off, in high contrast or when you turn it off in Settings, Ketvoice keeps its solid colours.
- **Full-screen player**: press F11 or the button in the player. The cover or the Canvas fills the left side, the lyrics scroll on the right, over the colours of the artwork. Esc brings the window back.
- **Taskbar preview**: hover Ketvoice in the taskbar to see the cover, with previous, play/pause and next buttons under it.
- The lyrics move like on the phone 8.2: lines settle into place on springs and the word being sung grows very slightly.

## Ketmix

- **Ketmix Studio on the computer**: see both songs as waves, move and stretch the transition, draw the volume of the music and the voice with key points, undo and redo, listen to a preview, and save presets. Share a transition with a code, and use a code made on the phone.
- Your Studio presets follow your Ketvoice account, so the ones you save on your phone are here too, and the other way round.
- **Voices ready ahead**: while the computer is plugged in, Ketvoice prepares the voice separation of your downloaded songs in the background, so the voice level slider and Ketmix vocal transitions start at once.

## Faster and lighter

- Ketvoice draws with Direct3D in step with your screen, for smoother scrolling and animations. If Direct3D cannot start, it falls back on its own.
- Less memory: covers in memory are capped and decoded no larger than needed, and Ketvoice gives unused memory back to Windows when it sits in the notification area.
- Ketvoice keeps a cache of its own code in its data folder after the first start, which Java reads at every later start.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-1.1.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over Ketvoice 1.0.0 and keeps your library, settings and downloads. It works on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown: choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. If you already use Ketvoice 1.0.0, it can also offer the update itself.
