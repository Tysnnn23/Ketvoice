# Ketvoice for Windows 2.0.1

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Sharper artwork, a Canvas that fills the player, everything in sync with your phone, and a Ketmix that mixes instead of echoing.

## Canvas and covers

- **The Canvas fills the player**: in the full player, full screen, album pages and Home, the Canvas now covers its whole area edge to edge, at your screen's real resolution (including Windows scaling), with the framing chosen for the player. It fades in smoothly from the cover while it loads.
- **Sharp covers that always show**: covers are requested at the size they are displayed, kept in a cache on your PC, and retried when a load fails.
- **Playlist covers**: a playlist shows its own picture, or a mosaic of its first songs' covers, like on the phone.
- **Custom artwork from Ketvoice**: the custom covers, Canvas and artist pictures published in Ketvoice now appear on Windows too, are ready at start-up, and refresh within minutes of being published.

## Everything in sync with your phone

- **Recently played and Statistics** now match your phone on the same account. Each play is counted once.
- **Discord**: your "show what I'm listening to" choice follows you, and the Connections page shows where Discord is connected.
- Favourites, playlists (including names made of a single emoji), artists and settings keep syncing.

## Ketmix

- **Far fewer echoes**: Ketmix now blends songs together most of the time; an echo only comes when it clearly suits, and never twice in a row. A song that stops at full volume is mixed out a little earlier instead.
- **Smoother transitions**: no swell in plain fades, a faster bass swap on the beat, no clicks in echoes.
- **The live mix view is gone.** Rating a transition up or down ("Ketmix learns") is still in the Ketmix tab and after each transition.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-2.0.1.msi` below and open it. It installs over any earlier Ketvoice for Windows and keeps your library, settings and downloads. It works on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown: choose "More info", then "Run anyway". If you already use Ketvoice for Windows, it can also offer the update itself.
