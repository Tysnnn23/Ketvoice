# Ketvoice 6.2.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Your Ketvoice account with your photo and your library on every device, a library that understands what you ask, a livelier player, a welcome for new listeners and a limited theme for each season.

## Your Ketvoice account

- **Create an account** with an email address and a password, or sign in with Discord: no code to type and no second step.
- **Your profile**: a name, a handle and a profile photo you crop yourself; your photo appears on Home, in your library, in search and in Settings.
- **Your library everywhere**: favourites, playlists, followed artists and settings follow you to every phone where you sign in, and deleting something on one device deletes it on the others.
- **Resume here**: Home offers to pick up the song you were playing on another device, at the same moment.
- Your account is private: only you can see your profile and your library, and you can delete the account and everything in it from Settings.

## A smarter library

- **Search the way you talk**: « chill French rap from the 2010s », « upbeat songs by Daft Punk »… Ketvoice reads the request on your phone and shows it as chips you can remove one by one.
- **Moods**: playlists made from your library by mood — calm, energy, party, focus, melancholy and good mood — each with its own cover, refreshable and savable as a playlist.
- **Duplicates**: copies of the same recording are found and merged in your playlists; a download is only deleted after you confirm.
- **Import playlists** from a public Spotify or Deezer link, with progress and the list of songs that were not found.

## A livelier player

- The cover drifts and zooms slowly while the music plays, with a soft light passing over it now and then.
- The background colours breathe with the bass and fade from one song to the next.
- When the song changes, the old cover steps back while the new one comes forward, and the title and artist cross-fade.
- Everything stays still with reduced motion, and is gentler in performance mode and in Ketvoice Lite.

## Welcome

- New listeners are welcomed on their first launch: choose the language, import playlists, and turn Ketmix and voice separation on or off. Updating keeps everything as it is.

## Seasonal themes

- A limited theme for every season, offered once when it begins and available in Settings → Appearance while it lasts: **Christmas** (11–31 December), **New Year** (31 December–2 January), **Valentine's Day** (7–15 February), **Spring** (20 March–20 April), **Summer** (21 June–31 August) and **Halloween** (20 October–2 November).
- The Vice City edition stays until 10 December.

## Also

- Shared song links open a new page with the large cover, a line of lyrics, the Ketogether session code and a button to open the song in Ketvoice.
- The website has a new release history page.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.2.0.apk` (phone), `Ketvoice-6.2.0-Lite.apk` (lighter phone app) or `Ketvoice-6.2.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
