# Ketvoice 8.0.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Glass that really bends light, menus that grow out of what you touch, a player that folds into the lyrics, Ketmix Studio and live statistics.

## Liquid Glass

- **Refractive glass**: the tab bar, the mini player, glass buttons, menus and sheets bend what passes under them, with a lens at the edges and a faint colour fringe on the rim. On Android 13 and up, the performance mode now keeps a lighter version of it instead of turning it off.
- **A tab bar that melts**: as you scroll, the tabs slide together into one capsule showing the current tab, following the speed of your scroll; tap it to bring them back.

## Motion

- **Menus that grow out of the button**: song menus, ⋯ menus, sheets and alerts open from the control you touched and fold back into it.
- **Springs everywhere**: page transitions, menus, selections and controls move on springs, so an animation you interrupt continues from where it is, without a jump.
- **Icons that react**: next and previous slide, the repeat icon changes shape, the Ketmix badge breathes during a mix, tabs bounce when you pick them and the bell rings when you turn on alerts.

## Player and lyrics

- **The player folds into the lyrics**: when you open the lyrics or the queue, the artwork slides into a small header next to the title in one continuous movement, and grows back when you close them. You can also drag the artwork up.
- **Duets**: when a featured singer takes over, their lines move to the other side, like Apple Music. Lines follow the singer information of the lyrics, or verse headers that name the featured artist.

## Ketmix Studio

- The mix editor opens full screen as Ketmix Studio, with undo and redo, a beat magnet and a visible “+ Point” button to place a volume point at the playhead.
- Pinch to zoom into the waveforms and pan with two fingers.
- **My presets**: save a transition you like, then apply it to other songs, rename or delete it.
- **Share a mix**: send a mix as a short code; your friend pastes it into their editor.

## Statistics

- A new Statistics page, always up to date: minutes this week, month or year compared with the period before, top artists, songs and genres, listening by hour, your streak, new artists and Ketmix mixes.
- Share “My top 5” and “My minutes” as images.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-8.0.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-8.0.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-8.0.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
