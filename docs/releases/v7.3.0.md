# Ketvoice 7.3.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Select and upgrade your downloads, import playlists from Apple Music, Tidal and SoundCloud, album pages with a floating cover, a player that breathes, and a share card made for Stories.

## Downloads

- The top of Downloads is cleaner: no more « Select » button.
- **Hold a song** and choose « Sélectionner » to start selecting, then tap to add more songs.
- **Hold a selected song** to act on the whole selection: Play, Add to a playlist, Delete, or **Download again in better quality**.
- Better quality is checked song by song first. The app tells you how many can be improved (for example 128 kb/s → FLAC), downloads them in the background and replaces a file only once the new one is complete. Lyrics, Canvas and separations stay.
- A song being downloaded shows its progress as a ring filling over its cover.

## Playlist import

- Import a playlist from **Apple Music**, **Tidal** or **SoundCloud** by pasting its public link, like Spotify and Deezer already. No account or key is needed.
- Songs are matched by ISRC and length when available. When a service shows only part of a playlist publicly, the app tells you how many songs it could read.

## Albums, search and player

- **Album and playlist pages** open on a large centred cover with a soft shadow in its own colour, with Play and Shuffle below. In Settings you can choose a full-screen Canvas header, or the cover floating over its Canvas.
- Play, Shuffle, links and badges on album and artist pages take the colour of the cover, kept readable.
- **Search** opens on the songs you played recently, then your artists, then large colourful category tiles.
- **The player breathes**: the artwork eases back when you pause and springs forward when you play, and the coloured background slows down with it.
- **About this song** in the ⋯ menu shows artists, album, year, label, genre, BPM, ISRC and audio quality.

## Ketmix and sharing

- During a Ketmix mix, the mini player shows both covers crossing, with a fine wave between them.
- **Share** opens a new card with the cover, title, artist and an optional lyric line, ready for an Instagram Story or any app.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-7.3.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-7.3.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-7.3.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
