# Ketvoice 6.1.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Listen together with Ketogether, place your own mix points in the Ketmix editor, see the sung line on your lock screen, discover new music every Monday — and a limited Vice City edition for the release of GTA VI.

## Ketogether

- **Listen together**: start a session and share its code, link or QR code; everyone hears the same song at the same moment, each on their own phone.
- **A shared queue**: guests propose songs from any song's menu; the host adds them, or lets everyone add and control playback.
- Reactions, a live pill in the player and the mini-player, and signed host messages so nobody can pretend to be the host.

## Ketmix

- **The Ketmix editor**: hold the Ketmix switch, or choose *Mix point…* in a song's menu. See both songs' waveforms with their beats, bars and phrases, drag the exit and entry points (they snap to the beat), set the overlap and the style, listen, and Ketmix remembers your points for every later mix.

## Lyrics

- **On the lock screen**: the line being sung, and the next one, in a silent notification you can see without unlocking (a Live Update on Android 16).
- **Clearer**: no blur and no halo on the words any more; only a note the singer holds glows a little.
- The large player widget shows the sung line too, and all widgets are redone in the glass look.

## Discover

- **Weekly discoveries**: about thirty songs you have not heard, chosen from what you listen to, renewed every Monday — on Home and in Android Auto.
- **A better radio**: artist and song radio without repeats, mixing new songs with ones you love.
- **Monthly Replay**: your minutes, top songs, artists and albums for each month, with its playlist.
- **A richer Wrapped**: listening clock, mood, discoveries and Ketmix pages, each one shareable as a story.

## Artist and album pages

- **Artists**: a large photo that drifts slowly, Play, Shuffle and Favourite, popular songs, the latest release, the discography, similar artists and the biography.
- **Albums**: explicit, downloaded and voice-separated marks on every song, the release date and total length, and more from the artist.

## Music that keeps playing

- Voice separation and Ketmix's heavy work now run in a process of their own: if the phone stops it, the music goes on.
- **Automatic resume**: if Android stops playback by itself, Ketvoice brings the same song back at the same moment (Settings → Playback; on by default). Allow *Alarms & reminders* when Settings offers it, for a resume within a minute.

## Ketvoice Lite

- A new, lighter APK of Ketvoice: the same features and effects, about a third of the size, tuned for modest phones. It installs over the regular app and keeps your library, settings and downloads.

## Vice City edition

- To celebrate the release of GTA VI on 19 November, a welcome screen greets you after updating, until 20 November.
- **Vice City theme**: night violet, sunset pink and orange, a soft sunset glow and neon edges, in Settings → Appearance until 10 December. A matching Vice City app icon is available until then too.
- Ketvoice is not affiliated with Rockstar Games.

## Also

- The tab bar always folds into a pill as you scroll, with the mini-player docked beside it.
- Sliders, rows, empty pages and loading lists keep the iOS 27 polish of the last beta.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.1.0.apk` (phone), `Ketvoice-6.1.0-Lite.apk` (lighter phone app) or `Ketvoice-6.1.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
