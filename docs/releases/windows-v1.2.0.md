# Ketvoice for Windows 1.2.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Make Ketvoice your own on the computer: your colours, font, text size and corners, lyrics in your style, the sidebar, Home, player buttons, library and menus you want. It also brings an equaliser for each pair of headphones, a choice of audio output, a lyrics editor and the newest Ketmix.

## Personalisation

- **Your colours**: pick the colours of the page, the cards, the text and the accent, or start from a preset. Ketvoice keeps text readable whatever you choose. Share a theme with a code, and use a code made on the phone.
- **Font, size and corners**: five fonts, text from 85 % to 130 %, corners from square to very round, and three list densities.
- **Lyrics in your style**: font, size, centred or left-aligned, the colour of the line being sung, how much the other lines blur and how much they move.
- Everything follows your Ketvoice account, so the look you set on your phone comes to the computer, and the other way round. Leave everything as it is and Ketvoice looks exactly as before.

## Layout

- **Sidebar**: choose and order its entries. Search, Downloads and Favourites can have entries of their own.
- **Home**: order and hide its sections.
- **Player bar**: choose two to five buttons on the right, in your order. Like, add to a playlist and download are new choices.
- **Library**: albums and artists as a grid or a list, and the order each page opens with.
- **Title menu**: order and hide its items.
- **Search in Settings**: type a few letters at the top of Settings to jump to the right option.

## Listening

- **Equaliser for each pair of headphones**: import a parametric profile file for your headphones, link it to an audio output, and Ketvoice corrects the sound whenever music plays there. Compare with and without the correction at any time.
- **Choose the audio output**: send music to any speaker or headset connected to the computer, straight from the player bar. Ketvoice remembers it and comes back to it when it is plugged in again.
- **Ketmix loop bridge**: Ketmix can now loop a bar to bridge two songs of very different tempo, and the bar loop of the phone plays on the computer too.
- Ketmix now knows where each song is sung, from its lyrics, and plans its transitions around the voice.
- **Downloaded playlists stay up to date**: when a playlist you downloaded gains new titles, Ketvoice offers to download them.
- Choose the order in which Ketvoice looks for lyrics.

## Lyrics

- Words fill at an even pace with a softer edge, held notes come to life letter by letter, and the lines around the one being sung are gently softened.
- **Lyrics editor**: time the lyrics of any song yourself, line by line or word by word, while it plays. Press Space to set each time, use the arrows to adjust, Ctrl+Z to undo, and preview the result before you save. You can also export the timed lyrics as a file.
- The player artwork and the Canvas take the height of their own shape, and the Canvas fills its column edge to edge.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-1.2.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over Ketvoice 1.1.0 or 1.0.0 and keeps your library, settings and downloads. It works on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown: choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. If you already use Ketvoice for Windows, it can also offer the update itself.
