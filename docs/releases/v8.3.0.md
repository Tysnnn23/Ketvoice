# Ketvoice 8.3.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Smoother lyrics that come alive on held notes, a player artwork sized to what it shows, and glass that is lighter, cleaner and more physical.

## Lyrics

- **Smoother word fill**: each word lights up at an even pace, with a soft leading edge instead of a hard line. A word lifts by how long it is sung, then settles back.
- **Held notes come alive**: on a long note, the letters swell one after another with a soft glow, then return to rest when the note ends.
- **Focus on the line being sung**: lines further away soften with a light blur, which clears as soon as you scroll the lyrics yourself. You can turn it off with “Lyrics blur” in the lyrics settings.
- Backing vocals open under the line with a small rise, and the three dots now also wait through every instrumental break of four seconds or more.

## Player

- **The artwork takes its own shape**: the cover or the Canvas fills the width of the player and takes the height that suits it, down to the song's title. A square cover is shown whole, and a tall Canvas loses far less.
- Moving from a cover to a Canvas, or from one song to the next, resizes the artwork smoothly.

## Glass

- **Lighter glass**: the blur behind the glass is computed on a smaller picture and scaled back up, so the tab bar, menus and sheets cost much less to draw while looking the same.
- **Cleaner glass**: menus, sheets and alerts now open over a still picture of the page, so what is behind them no longer shimmers while they grow. Over a playing Canvas the glass stays live.
- **A tab bar that moves like a drop**: when you change tab, the selection lifts off the bar, stretches with its speed, bends the glass under it and settles back flat.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-8.3.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-8.3.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-8.3.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
