# Ketvoice for Windows 2.0.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A new Ketvoice for the computer: a liquid glass window that moves with your screen's refresh rate, a far stronger Ketmix on desktop PCs, your account connected everywhere, and sources you add yourself.

## A new window

- **Liquid glass**: the sidebar, the player and the side panel float over the page in real glass that bends the light at its edges. A soft lens follows the cursor over buttons and tabs.
- **The player is an island** at the bottom of the window. Click it and it unfolds into the full player in one motion, cover and all.
- **A full-frame Home** opens on what you play most, with the rest below.
- **A side panel** for lyrics, the queue, the artist, Ketmix and listening together, open next to any page.
- **Smooth everywhere**: pages slide in on springs, scrolling glides, and animations follow 120 Hz and 144 Hz screens. Reduced motion is respected.
- **Compact player**: a small window that stays on top of the others. Press Ctrl+M or use the tray menu.
- Your colours, fonts, sidebar, Home and player buttons from 1.2 carry over to the new window.

## Ketmix, at full power on desktop PCs

- **Ketmix power**: Auto, Balanced or Maximum. On Auto, a desktop PC gets the large models below and a laptop keeps today's lighter ones, so its battery and fans stay calm.
- **Studio-quality voice separation**, a full beat and downbeat model, neural key detection, energy curves, song structure (intro, verse, chorus, bridge, outro) and sound similarity, so Ketmix picks better moments and better next songs.
- **Graphics card acceleration** when your card supports it, with a silent fallback to the processor.
- **Your library is prepared while the PC is idle**, so mixes are ready before you press play.
- **Live mix view**: watch both songs, their beats and every move Ketmix makes during a transition.
- **Ketmix learns** (optional): rate a transition up or down and Ketmix leans towards what you like.
- The models download once, on request, and are checked before use.

## Your account, connected

- **Connections page**: Discord, Last.fm and ListenBrainz in one place, with their status.
- **Discord**: show what you are listening to on your Discord profile, with a link to the song. You can hide the title.
- **Scrobbling** to Last.fm and ListenBrainz, kept offline and sent later when you are not connected.
- **Live sync**: pick up on the computer where you left off on the phone, and the other way round.
- **Connected devices**: see where you are signed in, move playback to another device, or sign a device out from here.
- **Sign in with a QR code**: scan it with your phone or type the code on the website; no password to type on the computer.
- **Several profiles on one PC**, each with its own library and settings.
- **No telemetry**: nothing about how you use Ketvoice is sent anywhere. A My data page shows what leaves your PC, exports everything your account holds, and can delete your account.

## Together

- **Listening rooms** on the computer, shared with phones: host or join with a code or a link, react, and let others add songs.
- **A profile of your own**: photo, banner, bio, colour, and what you are playing, your artists of the month and pinned playlists, each visible to you, your friends or everyone.
- **Friends and their activity** (optional): see what your friends are playing and join their room.
- **Share links** for songs, albums, playlists and profiles open in the app on Windows and Android, and show a clean page everywhere else.

## Sources and playback

- **Sources page**: add a source by the address of its manifest, test it, turn it on or off and set its order. Your sources follow your account. A catalogue lists sources you can add in one click.
- **Smart cache**: the songs you are likely to play next start almost instantly.
- **Exclusive mode** (optional): bit-perfect playback straight to your audio device.
- **True gapless** (optional): live albums and continuous mixes play with no gap at all.

## Lyrics and library

- **Word-by-word sync by AI** on desktop PCs: lyrics that only had lines get each word timed to the voice. You can always go back to the original timing.
- **More lyrics sources at once**, the best timing first, and your choice of source for each song.
- **Live translation and romanisation**, with the translation under each line if you want.
- **Floating lyrics** (optional): a transparent band on your desktop that lights up as the words are sung. Lock it and clicks go through it.
- **One-click import** of an outside playlist, with a report of what was found, what is uncertain and what is missing, and a way to fix each song.
- **Follow an outside playlist**: new songs are added for you or offered.
- **Drag and drop anywhere**: links, audio files, folders, lyrics files and source manifests.
- **Watched folders**: songs you add to your music folders appear in Ketvoice on their own.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-2.0.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over Ketvoice for Windows 1.2.0, 1.1.0 or 1.0.0 and keeps your library, settings and downloads. It works on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown: choose "More info", then "Run anyway". If you already use Ketvoice for Windows, it can also offer the update itself.
