# Ketvoice TV 1.1.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A big update for the big screen: a new player, a living Home, profiles for everyone in the house, and Ketvoice on the TV's own home screen.

## A new player

- The cover, or the song's Canvas, sits in a rounded frame on the left and the synced lyrics fill the right side, larger than before.
- Without lyrics, or once you hide them, the artwork moves to the centre, large, with the title under it and one control bar along the bottom. The two layouts glide into each other.
- The background is a soft field made from the colours of the cover. No video plays behind the player any more: the Canvas plays only inside its frame.
- The queue opens as a side panel, and Back takes you straight to the controls.

## Ketmix on the big screen

- During a mix the colours move from one song to the next, and the new cover fades in right on the bass swap.
- When Ketmix asks how a transition sounded, press Up for a thumbs up or Down for a thumbs down.
- "Mix this playlist" and "Mix this album": Ketmix measures the first songs, gently reorders the list so each song flows into the next, then plays it.
- On powerful TVs Ketmix now uses its deepest analysis, and Ketvoice TV offers to download the rhythm model once.

## A living Home

- The card you select shows its cover or artist photo large at the right of the screen, with its name above the rows.
- Opening an album, a playlist or an artist grows the card into the page, and Back shrinks it home.
- Artist pages fill the screen with the artist's photo, their story, popular songs, albums, singles, your own songs of theirs and similar artists.

## Who's listening?

- Each person in the house can have their own profile, signed in with their own Ketvoice account: their library, statistics, Ketmix settings and history stay separate.
- Pick your profile when Ketvoice TV opens, and add or remove profiles in Settings.

## Play on the TV

- From the phone or the computer, choose the TV in the devices list to move what you are playing to the TV. The TV starts playing at once and says where the music came from.

## Ketvoice around the TV

- The Ketvoice screensaver shows the cover of the song playing, its title and the time, and moves gently so nothing stays in one place. With no music it shows your favourite covers.
- Art frame mode turns the TV into a frame for the covers of the albums you play and like most, with artist photos in between. Open it from Home, Library or Settings.
- Continue listening and a Ketvoice channel with your playlists, mixes and recent albums appear on the TV's home screen while you are signed in. You can turn this off in Settings.
- Say "Hey Google, play an artist on Ketvoice" to start that artist's radio.
- Replay and Wrapped play as full-screen slides: your minutes, top artists, top songs, genres and listening hours, with music.

## Install

Ketvoice TV offers this update itself. To install it by hand, use the Downloader app with `https://github.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/releases/download/tv-v1.1.0/Ketvoice-TV-1.1.0.apk`, or run `adb install -r Ketvoice-TV-1.1.0.apk` from a computer. It installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your settings. Ketvoice TV works on Android TV 9 and later and on Google TV.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the SHA-256 of `Ketvoice-TV-1.1.0.apk`: compare it with `sha256sum Ketvoice-TV-1.1.0.apk` before installing with adb.
