# Ketvoice 5.9.2

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Lyrics videos to share, a performance mode that keeps the effects, and a like that no longer moves the player.

## New

- **Share lyrics as a video.** Hold a line in the lyrics, pick up to four, then *Video*: the title's Canvas blurred behind (or its cover's colours flowing), your lines in a frosted card that light up word by word in time with the song, the cover and the title under them. A story-sized MP4, with or without the Ketvoice logo. *Image* still makes the picture, which now holds up to four lines too.
- **A new performance mode** (Settings → Appearance): the effects stay visible and only what you barely notice is lightened — glass without its costly refraction, sharp lyrics instead of blurred far lines, the player's flowing colours at a quarter of the frames, images that appear without fading. Album Canvas keeps playing.

## Fixed

- **Liking a title no longer moves the player**: the heart's burst plays around the button without pushing the title, the artist and the buttons.
