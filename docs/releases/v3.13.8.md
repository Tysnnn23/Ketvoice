# Ketvoice 3.13.8

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

## Ketvoice Studio: real album management

- New **Albums** section. Search by album or artist, then open an album to see its
  tracks, its Canvas status and its candidates.
- Upload one **Canvas for the whole album**, or **one Canvas for a single track**
  from the same screen. The app downloads `canvasURL` directly, so the Studio and
  the app are synchronised on the next track load.
- **Remove a track from an album** or **add any of your other published tracks**
  to it. Adding reuses the album artist and cover; removing keeps the track
  published as a single.
- The song editor still offers the previous per-track Canvas with the
  "apply to the whole album" checkbox, for one-off changes.

## Artist pages

- The play button now reads **Listen / Écouter** instead of the settings word
  "Playback", and the biography toggle reads **Show less / Afficher moins**.
- `EP` and `Compilation` are translated, and every artist-page label is present in
  all 10 language tables (French is the source language).
- Artist logos stay opt-in (Settings → Apparence → Logos d'artistes), as before;
  the broker now paces MusicBrainz so back-to-back lookups stop returning 429,
  which was why logos appeared broken.

## Discord

- Opening Discord now tries the normal launcher, the leanback launcher, the
  `discord://` scheme, and a `discord.com` hand-off. The panel only reports a
  failure if every route fails.
- Presence still opens Discord first, waits, then connects. The OAuth2 redirect to
  register remains displayed in the advanced section.

## Player

- Removed the oversized first-letter fallback that appeared on the artwork when a
  cover was missing, along with its slight baseline offset.

## Validation

- Unit tests, release builds, lint and the credential scan run before publication.
  No phone, Quest or accelerated emulator was available: the Studio album actions,
  the Discord launch, the glow and the Canvas playback are **not
  hardware-validated**.
