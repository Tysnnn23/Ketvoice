# Ketvoice 4.11.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A more personal Home, mixes made from your listening, and a redesigned Downloads screen.

## Home

- **A greeting, not a slogan.** The title follows the time of day.
- **Shortcuts to what you have.** Shuffle play, Favourites, Offline and History appear
  only when there is something behind them.
- **A little different at each launch.** The first personal shelf always leads; the
  album row and the other shelves take turns from one launch to the next, and nothing
  moves while you browse.

## Your mixes

After ten plays, Home proposes up to four mixes built from the listening history kept
on the device:

- **{Artist} Mix** — your tracks by an artist you play a lot, then their essentials.
- **In the style of {Artist}** — tracks by similar artists from the public catalogue.
- **On repeat** — what you play the most.
- **Rediscover** — tracks you played before and have not heard recently.

Your most played artist always keeps a mix; the others rotate at each launch. Any mix
can be kept with **Save as playlist**. Artist and style mixes need a connection when
opened; On repeat and Rediscover work offline.

## Downloads

- A summary card: titles, size, total play time, the lossless share of your storage,
  free space on the device, and the offline capsule.
- **Titles, Albums or Artists** in one segmented control, with a new Artists view.
- **Sort** by most recent, title, artist or size.
- Each title shows its real stored format as a badge (lossless stands out), its size,
  and small marks for saved lyrics and Canvas.
- Transfer-state filters only appear while something is being transferred.
