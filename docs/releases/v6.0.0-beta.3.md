# Ketvoice 6.0.0 Beta 3

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

The voice level now works on every song.

## The voice of a song

- **The voice slider is on every song.** On the lyrics screen, beside the lyrics and above the translation button, it appears with the player's controls (never in full-screen lyrics), whatever you are listening to.
- **Touch it and Ketvoice separates the song you are hearing**, on the phone (the time depends on the phone and the song's length): a small spinner shows at the top of the slider meanwhile, and the voice follows the slider as soon as it is done. You can set the level right away; it applies when the song is ready.
- **The first time, Ketvoice offers to download the separation model** (about 39 MB, once). After that, separating needs no network for downloaded songs.
- **Once you have turned the voice down, it stays down**: each next song is separated as it starts.
- Works on downloaded songs and on most streamed songs. A few streams made of many small files cannot be read whole; download those songs to adjust their voice.
- A song separated once stays separated: Ketmix uses it for its mixes too.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.0.0-beta.3.apk` (phone) or `Ketvoice-6.0.0-beta.3-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
