# Ketvoice 7.1.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketmix mixes more carefully, glass that stays readable, a true black theme, a compact widget and a radio that does not repeat itself.

## Ketmix

- **Endings are kept**: Ketmix no longer cuts real music off the end of a song to start a mix. When no good point fits, it makes a shorter transition instead.
- **False endings and hidden tracks**: a song that goes silent and starts again is no longer mixed inside the silence.
- **Tempo matching only when it is safe**: songs are played in time only when both beat grids are steady, and not under a voice when the tempo would have to move more than 3.5 %.
- **Songs that do not go together** change with a short fade instead of a DJ blend.
- **Two voices at once**: when two sung lines still meet, the outgoing song steps back.
- **Steady volume**: volume leveling holds still during a transition, so there is no dip or bump.
- **Listen in the editor** plays the next song again: in the transition editor, « Listen » now brings in the incoming song, with and without the mix.
- The DJ set mode is removed.

## Glass and theme

- Glass is kept for navigation: the tab bar, the mini player, the sidebar, menus and top bars. Buttons and cards in pages are solid, and glass is never stacked on glass.
- **Clear or Tinted**: a new choice above the transparency slider. Tinted is clearly more opaque, and it turns on by itself when Android's high-contrast text is on.
- Menus and sheets are much more opaque than the tab bar, so their text stays readable over any artwork.
- **Black**: a new switch under Theme for true black pages on OLED screens.

## Layout and more

- The mini player keeps « Next » when the tab bar folds, and a swipe on it changes song.
- Titles, artists and albums sorted by name get an A–Z index on the right edge.
- **New widget « Commandes »**: previous, play/pause and next, even at its smallest size.
- The radio and autoplay do not replay a song you heard in the last four hours.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-7.1.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-7.1.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-7.1.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
