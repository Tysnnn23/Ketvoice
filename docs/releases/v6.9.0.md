# Ketvoice 6.9.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A new Ketmix editor you can shape and hear, and three things only Ketmix does.

## The Ketmix editor, redone

- **One timeline**: the ending of the outgoing song lies over the start of the next one, on the same clock. The transition is a box: drag it to move the mix, pull its edges to make it longer or shorter. It still lands on the beat and the bar.
- **What you see is what plays**: the volume, the bass handoff and the filter are drawn over the waves, with the bar numbers, where each voice sings and where the lyric lines start.
- **Presets**: Auto, then every Ketmix style, each one heard at once.
- **Details**: the shape of the volume, how long the bass takes to change hands, a high-pass or low-pass filter, and whether the tempo may be matched.
- **Listen anytime**: « Listen » plays just the transition, even when that song is not playing, and « Without mix » plays the same moment as a plain cut to compare. What you were listening to resumes after.
- **Save** keeps your changes for that pair of songs; closing with changes asks first. « … » brings back Ketmix's own choice or applies your settings to the whole queue.
- Pinch or use − and + to zoom.

## Only on Ketmix

- **Mixes that respect the lyrics**: Ketmix reads the synced lyrics. A blend never cuts a sung line: the voice leaves at the end of its line, and the next song comes in just before its own line.
- **Bar loop**: when a song ends on a fade, Ketmix can loop a clean instrumental bar on the beat while the next song comes in.
- **Variety**: after two tempo blends in a row, an echo or a rise takes a turn when the songs allow it.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.9.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-6.9.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-6.9.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
