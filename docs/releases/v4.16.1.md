# Ketvoice 4.16.1

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Curated artwork from Ketvoice, everywhere you look.

## Artwork

- **Curated covers, Canvas and artist photos.** When Ketvoice gives a title, an album
  or an artist a refreshed cover, Canvas or photo, it now appears across the app: the
  player and mini-player, lists, search, Home, album and artist pages, the lock screen
  and the widget. They arrive without an app update and are kept for offline use.
- An album's artwork also applies to each of its titles, and a title's Canvas follows
  its album when the title has none of its own.
- Artist pages fit a wide photo to its real shape instead of a square crop.
