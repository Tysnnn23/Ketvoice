# Ketvoice 5.4.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Longer, tempo-matched Ketmix blends that start under the last sung bars; your accent colour everywhere; a new shelf of recent titles.

## Ketmix (beta)

- **Longer blends, more often in time.** Blends now run up to 16 bars (64 beats, about 30 seconds at 128 BPM), and two titles are played in time when their tempos are within 5 % (discreet), 8 % (balanced) or 12 % (DJ) — the tempos meet half-way, so each title is only stretched by about half of that.
- **Under the last words.** In the balanced and DJ settings, a blend may start under the last two (balanced) or four (DJ) sung bars of the outgoing title: its voice steps back — the centre of the stereo image, where a lead vocal sits, is lowered between 220 Hz and 4.5 kHz while the instruments carry on — instead of the mix waiting for the very end. A title that ends on a last note at full level still keeps its ending whole.
- **Mix journal.** Settings → Playback → Ketmix now lists the last transitions — what was played or why nothing was, the tempos and keys measured, how sure the analysis was, and whether a plain fade had to be used — with *Copy the journal* to send it when a mix sounds wrong.

## Colours

- **Your accent colour is respected everywhere.** With *Cover colours* on, only album pages, artist pages and the player take their colours from the artwork; the rest of the app keeps the accent you chose.
- **Album pages take the colour their artwork is mostly made of**, not its most vivid detail (a black-and-white cover with a small gold detail now gives a neutral page), and follow the cover as soon as a Canvas is switched off. Light pages stay light under a dark cover.

## Home

- **Recent titles as a shelf of large covers**, each lifted by a shadow of its own colour, with a play button and the bars of the title playing; a long press opens a title's menu. The layout button switches to a grid of covers.
