# Ketvoice 9.0.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketvoice becomes yours: your colours, your font, your corners, your tabs, your Home, your player buttons and your menus. Plus a headphone equalizer that follows your headphones, and an editor to time lyrics yourself. Left untouched, Ketvoice looks and behaves exactly as before.

## Personalisation

A new “Personalisation” page in Settings, with a live preview at the top.

- **Your theme**: set the background, surfaces, text and accent colours for light and dark mode with a full colour picker, or start from seven ready-made themes. Text always stays readable: a colour that would be too faint is adjusted just enough, and the page tells you.
- **Share a theme**: copy your theme as a short code and paste a friend's code to try theirs.
- **Font, size and corners**: choose among five fonts, make the text smaller or larger without changing the rest of your phone, go from square to very round corners, and pick compact, normal or airy lists.
- **Lyrics your way**: their font, size and alignment, the colour of the word being sung, and how strong the blur and the word movement are.
- **Layout**:
  - Choose and reorder two to five tabs, now including Downloads and Favourites.
  - Show, hide and reorder every section of Home; a long press on the greeting opens the editor right there.
  - Choose the buttons of the player's bottom row and up to two extra buttons on the mini player, make the artwork a little smaller or larger, and turn the Canvas off in the player.
  - Pick list or grid and a default order for your playlists, albums, artists and songs, reorder or hide the items of a song's menu, and choose what a swipe left or right on a song does.
- “Reset everything” and “Reset the layout” bring back the original look in one tap.

## Headphone EQ

- Import a headphone correction (the common parametric EQ text format, from a file or pasted), link it to your headphones, and Ketvoice applies it every time they are connected, Ketmix blends and the voice level included. A toggle lets you hear the difference.

## Lyrics editor

- “Edit lyrics…” in a song's menu and in the lyrics sources: paste or load the words, then tap in rhythm to time each line, or each word in word mode. Nudge a time, undo, and preview the result in the real lyrics view.
- Save the timing for yourself, or export it as an LRC file.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-9.0.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-9.0.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-9.0.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
