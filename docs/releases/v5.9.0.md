# Ketvoice 5.9.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Wrapped, four widgets, a ten-band equalizer, lyrics in the manner of Apple Music and a player whose colours flow.

## New

- **Ketvoice Wrapped.** Replay → *Wrapped*: your year as stories — the minutes you listened, your number 1 artist and your songs on repeat, over the flowing colours of your most played cover — and a story-sized picture to share. Years are counted from this version on; until this year holds enough plays, Wrapped covers everything Ketvoice counted since you started, and says so.
- **Four home-screen widgets**: a large Liquid Glass player (blurred cover, progress and controls), *Your week*, the mix of the moment (one tap plays it) and small 2×1 controls.
- **A ten-band equalizer** from 31 Hz to 16 kHz: drag the curve itself, or pick a preset (Bass, Vocal, Night, Brilliance, Rock, Electro). On most phones the ten bands are applied exactly, with a limiter so that boosts never clip; your previous settings are carried over.
- **Normalise volume** (Settings → Playback): every title at a similar perceived loudness, adjusted slowly so that nothing pumps within a song.
- **Ketmix on skip**: with Ketmix on, skipping to the next title is a short fade instead of a hard cut.
- **Cover colour** (Settings → Appearance): the accent colour of the whole app follows the cover playing.

## Design

- **The player's colours flow.** Without a Canvas, the player's background is three blurred copies of the cover turning slowly in opposite directions, as in Apple Music.
- **Lyrics in the manner of Apple Music**: each word rises a little as it is sung, held notes glow, and the sung line breathes very slightly with the bass.
- **Shared lyrics** now lie over the blurred cover.
- **A simpler Home**: the large *For you* card and the four shortcut tiles are gone; *Your week* stays.

## Faster

- The lyrics index and the launcher icon check are read beside the first screen instead of before it, and Discord starts after the first frame.
