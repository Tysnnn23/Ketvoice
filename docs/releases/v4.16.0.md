# Ketvoice 4.16.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A Home that knows you a little better, a more polished player, and moments to share.

## Home

- **For you.** A large card at the top changes with what you were doing: a paused
  title to pick up where you left off, a new release from an artist you play, or the
  mix that suits the hour (your morning, your afternoon, your evening mix, for the night).
- **New from your artists.** The latest albums and singles of the artists you play
  most, from the public catalogue.
- **Your week.** Listening time, plays, artist and title of the last seven days, kept
  on the device. It appears once there is a week to tell.
- **Shelves that learn.** The shelves you open most move up; the ones you never open
  move down. The first personal shelf keeps its place, and nothing moves while you browse.
- **Editorial titles.** Section titles are larger, and a chevron shows when a section
  opens a full page (Recent, Pinned, catalogue albums).

## Player

- Paused, the artwork steps back with a small bounce; playing, it comes forward again.
- The progress bar swells under the finger and settles back when released.
- The colour background behind the player moves more slowly and reacts more gently.
- The mini-player shows a fine progress line in the accent colour, and its cover plays
  the title's Canvas when there is one.

## Lyrics

- **Cascade style** in Settings → Appearance → Lyrics style: the sung line alone stands
  forward, the others step back and follow it in a wave. The Ketvoice style stays the
  default.
- **Share a moment.** Long-press one or two lines to make a vertical card for a story:
  the lines, the cover and its colours, shared with a link that opens the title in
  Ketvoice. The title menu also has **Share a moment**, with the line being sung.

## Library

- Playlists and albums show as a grid of large covers in their own views.

## Validation

Host tests: 642 mobile and 641 Quest tests pass, with lint and the UI text audit.
