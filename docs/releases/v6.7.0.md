# Ketvoice 6.7.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketmix blends more like a DJ would, Ketvoice Lite gets lighter again, and the GTA VI welcome screen is back.

## Ketmix (BETA)

- **The blend fits the pair**: two songs that need a big tempo change or sit at very different levels get a shorter blend.
- **Never two voices at once**: a blend no longer starts under the last sung words while the next song is already singing.
- **On the bar**: when the next song starts with a pickup, its first bar now lands on the phrase, and the basses swap on a downbeat.
- **Even levels**: going into a quieter song, the outgoing one eases down first, so the level never drops or jumps.
- **Albums in order**: songs that run into each other (applause, a held chord) and albums without a beat are left as recorded.

## Ketvoice Lite

- Starts faster: in French nothing is read for translations, and other languages load only their own.
- On Android 11 the frosted glass is no longer recomputed while nothing changes behind it.
- On tablets the player bar at the bottom no longer redraws the whole bar as the song advances.

## Vice City

- The « Enjoy GTA VI » welcome screen is back as it was: the sunset picture, the glass panel and the sunset button. The theme itself keeps its quiet look.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.7.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-6.7.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-6.7.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
