# Ketvoice 4.2.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

## Album artwork without the white wash

- Album artwork begins at the window's top edge, behind the floating controls.
  The list no longer reserves a blank toolbar strip above it.
- Removed the large white/background dissolve painted over the lower image.
  A short colour transition sits **below** the artwork instead.
- Album accents are sampled from the album being viewed, not the song playing
  elsewhere. The album Canvas toggle stays independent from the player toggle;
  the video pauses when the hero scrolls out of view.
- Top controls sample the scrolling page instead of a disconnected blurred wash.

## One player opening and closing animation

- Replaced the stacked full-screen slide, separate flying cover and off-screen
  dismissal with one rounded-surface reveal from the mini-player's actual bounds.
- The player remains full-size behind the animated mask: text and video are not
  stretched between aspect ratios, and no duplicate artwork flies over the screen.
- Closing reverses the reveal. The handle drag drives the same presentation;
  short drags settle back. Rapid open/close reversals and reduced motion are covered
  by host regression tests. Quest retains its landscape two-column player.
- The mini-player and navigation now keep their component identity while their
  containers resize and move. Narrow phone windows retain the readable expanded
  layout instead of squeezing every control into a single row.

## More restrained glass and useful actions

- Reduced broad refraction, chromatic separation, heavy rim highlights and the
  milky blur on optical controls. Content menus use a calm matte surface.
- Appearance includes a **glass preview** on the current cover (or the Ketvoice
  logo when no cover exists): compare Clear, Frosted and Opaque without saving
  until Apply is pressed. Performance and reduced-blur fallbacks remain available.
- Track menus put Favorite, Play next and Download in a top quick-action row.
  Play/pause uses one interpolated glyph instead of crossfading two icons; favorite
  hearts and download paused/Wi-Fi/completed states have consistent feedback.
- Swipe album and search-result rows to Play next or Add to queue. A confirmation
  offers Undo for the **inserted queue occurrence**, even with duplicate songs.
  Undo never interrupts a song that has since become current. The same actions
  are available through accessibility actions. No swipe downloads or deletes media.

## Optional Stats info, not a permanent audio badge

- The audio readout is behind **Appearance → Stats info**, off by default, inside
  the player only. It reports the decoder-selected MIME format, sample rate,
  channel count and bitrate **when available**, the source, quality-search state
  and stream-error fallback. Unknown values remain unknown.
- A provider's format label alone is not used to claim lossless playback.

## Download controls that act on the transfer

- Pause/resume, cancel and retry actions; failures and cancelled tasks remain in
  the download screen rather than vanishing. Remaining bytes are shown only when
  the server supplies a total.
- Pause suspends further reads. Interrupted transfers request Range with a strong
  ETag or Last-Modified validator; inconsistent ranges or changed validators cannot
  be appended. A source refusing Range is restarted instead of corrupting the file.
- Optional Wi-Fi-only downloads wait before requesting a stream and before reading
  further audio chunks. The progress artwork distinguishes pause and Wi-Fi wait.
- These controls apply to the **current app process**, not an Android persistent
  download service. Pause is not a promise of resume after force-stop/reboot, and
  already-buffered bytes may arrive before a pause or connectivity change is seen.

## Validation and limitations

- Mobile/Quest JVM tests and native software host rendering; tests include reveal
  reversals, top-edge album bounds, optional stats, preference persistence, duplicate
  queue Undo, paused transfer, HTTP range/validator resume and the Wi-Fi gate.
- Transfer tests use in-memory HTTPS connection fixtures, **not live provider
  downloads**. Instrumentation APKs are compiled, not run on hardware.
- Release assets include signed Mobile and Quest APKs, checksums and validation
  metadata. Publication verifies anonymous downloaded bytes against local SHA-256.
- No connected phone, emulator or physical Quest was available. Hardware glass
  refraction/blur, Canvas decoding, gesture feel, device playback/downloads and
  Discord account consent are **not device-validated**. No FPS guarantee is made.
- French plus the ten other interface languages are retained; new interface copy
  includes translations for all ten non-French languages. No group listening or
  publisher-authorization changes are included. Existing playback-speed changes
  and upstream signing tooling are preserved.
