# Ketvoice 5.9.1

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Your own lyrics files, an app that takes the colour of what plays, truer cover colours, Discord that stays linked, and Ketmix mixes that keep voices apart.

## New

- **Custom lyrics.** In the player's lyrics sources, *Custom* opens your files: pick a lyrics file and Ketvoice uses it for that title from then on. LRC (with word timing and several times per line), TTML, SRT, WebVTT, ASS/SSA (with karaoke timing), YRC, JSON from the usual lyrics services and plain text are read; the format is recognised from the content. Choose another source to set it aside, *Change file* or *Remove* at any time.
- **Background from the cover** (Settings → Appearance, off by default): the background of the whole app takes the colour of what is playing, as an album page does.
- **Separate vocals (experimental)** (Settings → Ketmix): during a mix the next title comes in as its instrumental and its vocals join just before it sings, while the vocals of the title playing fade before the next one starts singing — two voices never sing over each other. The separation happens in real time, so nothing is computed ahead or stored.
- **Add a whole album or several titles to a playlist**: a new button on album pages, and *Add to a playlist* in the selection of the library and of the queue. Titles already in the playlist are not added twice.

## Better

- **Cover colours.** Album, artist and player colours now come from the main hue of the cover: a colourful cover on a dark or grey ground no longer turns grey, and two colours are no longer averaged into a third one (red and blue into purple, yellow and blue into green).
- **Discord stays linked.** A linked Discord account is signed in again when the app starts and after an update, instead of asking to link again.
