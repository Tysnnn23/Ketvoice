# Ketvoice 5.3.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketmix mixes on the phrase and loops a title into itself; Liquid Glass forms, bends and settles more naturally; a queue you can edit by the handful.

## Ketmix (beta)

- **Mixes start on a phrase.** Ketmix now finds where the music turns a page — the bar where a part comes in or drops out, every eight bars — and starts the next title there, as a DJ would. Echoes leave on the last phrase boundary before the end.
- **Better bars.** When the bass does not tell the bars apart, the first beat of a bar is found where the most new sound comes in from one beat to the next.
- **Layered blends.** In a blend the incoming title now arrives highs first, then mids, and its bass last, on the swap: one bass line at a time, all the way through.
- **Titles on repeat are mixed too.** With *Mix a title on repeat too* (on by default), a repeated title blends into its own start on the beat instead of starting again after a gap. Its overlap is kept short, so the title never colours itself.
- **Choose the style.** Settings → Playback → Ketmix → *Transition style*: Automatic, Matched to the tempo, Echo, Filters or Fade. Singing is never covered, whatever the style, and Ketmix falls back to another style when the chosen one does not suit the two titles.
- **See the mix.** While two titles play together, an animated strip above the progress bar shows the outgoing title fading over the incoming one, the playhead, the moment the bass changes hands, and the measured tempo and key.
- **Feel the swap.** *Vibrate on the bass swap* (off by default) gives a light vibration when the bass passes to the next title, while the app is on screen.

## Liquid Glass

- **A softer bezel.** In the Liquid 27 style, the edge of the glass is now a squircle rather than a circular arc: the bend leaves the flat centre gently and rises sharply at the rim, without a visible ring.
- **True Fresnel.** Edges reflect more the more edge-on they are (Schlick's approximation).
- **Glass that forms.** Glass now materialises by bending the light in over half a second instead of simply appearing.
- **Calmer motion.** A released control settles with one small overshoot instead of wobbling.
- **Switches become lenses.** A switch's thumb widens into a clear lens while the finger is on it, then settles back.
- **Readable edges.** Where content scrolls under the status bar or the floating bar, the page colour rises over it with an eased curve, in every glass style.

## Queue and shuffle

- **Select several titles.** Long-press a title in the queue (or tap *Select*), tick more, then *Play next* or *Remove* them at once. Both can be undone.
- **Shuffle with fewer repeats** (on by default): titles heard recently are kept for later and two titles by the same artist do not follow each other when it can be avoided.

## Night

- In the dark theme, pages coloured by a cover or a Canvas keep their hue but never go above a dim level, so a bright cover does not light up the screen at night.
