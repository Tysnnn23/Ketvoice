# Ketvoice 9.1.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A Ketmix update: mixes that follow the shape of each song, transitions that sound smoother and stay in time, and a Ketmix that can learn what you like.

## Ketmix follows the song

- Ketmix now finds the intro, verses, choruses, bridge and outro of the songs it prepares (on Balanced and Maximum analysis).
- It leaves a song at the end of a chorus or where the outro starts instead of in the middle of a verse, and never cuts into the first bar of a chorus.
- Cuts and drops land where the next song's intro ends, or on its first chorus.
- Your own mix points from the Ketmix editor still come first, and songs without sections mix as before.

## Smoother transitions

- No more dip in loudness just before the bass changes hands: both songs now meet at the same, fuller level.
- Two songs blended in time stay locked together to the end of the blend, with no slow drift between the beats.
- Filter crossings fade out the outgoing song more evenly.

## Ketmix learns from you

- New option in Settings › Ketmix: "Ketmix learns from your ratings" (off by default).
- After a transition, the player asks whether you liked it, with 👍 and 👎. You can also rate the last transition from Settings.
- Ketmix then leaves out the kinds of transitions you turned down when it chooses by itself. A style you pick yourself is never changed.
- What Ketmix learns stays on your phone, and you can make it forget everything at any time.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-9.1.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-9.1.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-9.1.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
