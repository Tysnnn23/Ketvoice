# Ketvoice 6.8.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketmix can now use a small AI model to find the beat and the bars of your songs.

## Ketmix (BETA)

- **A rhythm model, on request**: in Settings → Ketmix, « Precise rhythm » downloads a small AI model (about 19 MB). It finds the beats and the first beat of every bar, so a blend can land on the bar instead of next to it.
- Without the model, Ketmix works exactly as before. With it, a title is analysed by the model when it is confident, and by Ketmix's own analysis otherwise.
- On Ketvoice Lite and on phones with little memory, the model works only while the phone charges.
- The model is Beat This! (JKU Linz, MIT license), converted by Ketvoice.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.8.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-6.8.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-6.8.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
