# Ketvoice 5.0.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Automix: titles flow into each other like a mix.

## Automix

- **Turn it on from the queue or from Settings.** A new button at the bottom left of the
  queue (two overlapping rings) switches Automix on and off; Settings → Automix has the
  same switch. It is off until you turn it on.
- **A real mix, not only a fade.** Towards the end of a title, the next one is brought in
  at the same tempo (its speed follows the outgoing title, its pitch is kept), beats
  aligned, and the bass passes from one title to the other on a beat. Once the outgoing
  title is gone, the new one glides back to its own tempo.
- **Lyrics are respected.** Synchronised lyrics tell where the singing stops and starts:
  the mix waits for the end of the last sung line, and a transition is shortened rather
  than leaving two voices singing over each other.
- **The right transition for each pair.** A beat mix when the tempos are close; a plain
  crossfade when they are too far apart or when there is no steady beat; and when a title
  ends on a last note at full level, that note is left alone and only the silence after
  it is removed.
- **Some listening is never mixed.** Albums played in order, a repeated title, the sleep
  timer and bit-perfect output keep their titles exactly as they are.
- **Three intensities.** Discreet (short transitions, the title plays to its end),
  Balanced, and DJ (longer mixes that start as soon as the singing is over).
- **You see it happen.** A "Mixing" badge breathes with the beat over the artwork, the two
  covers dissolve into each other, a second fine line runs above the progress bar, and
  the mini-player shows the same dissolve.
- **On the device.** The tempo, the beats and the shape of each title's start and end
  are measured on the device and kept, so a title is only measured once.

## Look

- **One capsule for a page's actions.** On album and artist pages, the actions at the top
  sit side by side in a single capsule of glass instead of separate bubbles; the album's
  Download and Refresh share one too.
