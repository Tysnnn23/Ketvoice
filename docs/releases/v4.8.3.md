## Collapsed chrome: Home — mini-player — Search

- While a page is scrolled, the floating chrome becomes a single liquid-glass row: the **Home** button on the left, a **smaller mini-player** in the middle and **Search** on the right.
- The three-tab capsule morphs into the Home button (its width and height animate into a 52 dp glass circle). Tapping it returns to Home; scrolling back up restores the three tabs.
- The mini-player shrinks continuously rather than switching abruptly: the pill height goes from 54 to 48 dp, the cover from 44 to 32 dp, and the title/artist fade out. Play and Next keep 48 dp touch targets and remain visible in the compact pill.
- The capsule, mini-player and Search button share the same spring, and their horizontal positions are derived from the same fraction, so the row moves as one piece. The Search circle goes from 60 to 52 dp and keeps its right margin.
- On narrow panes below 380 dp the previous condensed layout is retained, so the three tabs are never squeezed into unreadable icons.

## Validation limits

No phone or Quest was connected for this build. A Robolectric host test at 400 dp asserts that Home is left of the mini-player, the mini-player is left of Search, the transport keeps a 48 dp touch target and the tabs are gone in the collapsed state. Host Skia does not prove the on-device liquid-glass refraction, the spring feel, GPU frame rate or physical Quest behaviour. JVM tests, lint, signatures and checksums are not device validation.

For update compatibility these APKs use the existing public Android debug signing identity, not a private production signing key. Never uninstall an existing installation to resolve a signature conflict without backing up its data.
