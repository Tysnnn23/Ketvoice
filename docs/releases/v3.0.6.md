# Ketvoice 3.0.6 — AutoMix (BETA)

**Mobile/tablet and Quest · versionCode 31**

- Enable AutoMix in Settings → Playback. It remains disabled by default. When
  enabled, a dedicated AutoMix tab offers automatic or custom maximum crossfade
  (1–30 seconds) and Low, Medium, Standard or High analysis performance.
- The player pre-resolves the next stream and prepares a second ExoPlayer before
  the end of the current song. Two independent audio streams overlap with a
  gain-limited crossfade, without pausing the outgoing deck. Pausing, seeking,
  changing tracks or disabling AutoMix cancels the transition. Manually skipping
  to an already prepared next song uses a short fade where possible.
- Bounded local/remote PCM samples are analyzed off the UI thread to estimate
  tempo, energy and mid-band vocal risk. If tempo estimates are confident and
  compatible, beat phases can align without changing playback speed. Analyses
  are cached on disk; when analysis fails, a plain equal-power fade is used.
- The full-screen player shows an unobtrusive AutoMix indicator, overlapping
  covers and transition progress; reduced-motion mode avoids decorative motion.
  Mobile, tablet and Quest retain their existing player layouts.
- The owner Studio sign-in redirect from 3.0.5 is also included. Links requested
  by the Android app still target the Android app.

This beta uses spectral heuristics, not vocal stem separation or time-stretching.
Some songs with unavailable duration, expiring streams, unsupported codecs or
late network responses use ordinary next-track playback. Unit tests and
packaging checks are not physical audio validation. On-device playback,
headphone focus changes and Quest output require owner verification.
