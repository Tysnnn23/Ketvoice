# Ketvoice 5.8.1

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A fix for 5.8.0, in which titles, downloaded or streamed, could stop starting and the app could stutter.

## Fixed

- **Playback and smoothness.** The optimised build introduced in 5.8.0 is withdrawn: Ketvoice is built again as in 5.7.0, with every feature of 5.8.0 kept.
- Opening a page from a cover no longer redraws the whole page at every frame of its animation.
- The search by lyrics no longer rewrites its index each time lyrics already known are shown again.

## New look

- **A new Liquid Glass app icon**: a "K" of clear, iridescent glass on black (Settings → Appearance → App icon → Liquid Glass).
