# Ketvoice 3.12.1

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

## Canvas framing

- Restore the banner-era normal Canvas framing. The clip now fills the whole
  artwork region edge to edge instead of being letterboxed, so the wide side
  bands reported after 3.12.0 are gone.
- Keep the blurred cover ambience behind the player, the shared dissolve between
  tracks and the same artwork-region geometry. Fullscreen remains reserved for
  verified portrait 9:16 clips and Quest stays a landscape 2D window.
- Remove the extra blurred cover drawn inside the artwork square: it duplicated
  the scene ambience and made the video look inset.

## Reverb

- Route audio through decoded PCM even when the device advertises encoded
  passthrough or offload. Those paths bypass every Media3 audio processor, which
  is why Reverb could be enabled without being heard on some phones.
- Deepen the room: longer damped tail, stronger wet mix and a DC blocker so the
  feedback network does not build sub-bass rumble that eats headroom.
- Apply Reverb changes through a MediaSession custom command in addition to the
  in-process setting, and show whether the audio stream is actually being
  processed while the slider is open.
- Keep multichannel passthrough intact: the reverb only accepts mono and stereo,
  so surround formats are not silently refused.

## Liquid glass

- Give the navigation bar and the player's two-tab pill a dedicated optical glass
  material: lower frosting, stronger edge refraction and a thinner tint, so the
  sampled page reads through instead of a matte slab.
- Sample the player footer from its own backdrop layer, so the navigation pill
  refracts the scene instead of the footer that contains it.

## Discord

- The Ketvoice Discord application is registered (Application ID
  `1566454532890820668`). The redirect to declare in OAuth2 is
  `discord-1566454532890820668:/authorize/callback`.
- Account linking and live listening presence are still **not shipped**. The
  official Android Social SDK is distributed only through the signed-in Discord
  developer portal, so its activation, terms and AAR/header download cannot be
  completed from this repository. No linked state, fake presence or non-functional
  button is included.

## Validation limits

- Unit tests, release assembly, lint and synthetic audio fixtures are not
  physical listening, hardware video decoding, GPU performance, real Discord
  linking or signed owner-session tests. No phone, Quest or accelerated emulator
  was available in this environment; the reported checks are the ones that ran.
