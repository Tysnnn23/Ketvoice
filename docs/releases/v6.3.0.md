# Ketvoice 6.3.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Shared playlists, a public profile, Air Ket to move your whole Ketvoice to a new phone, Canvas that stay on your phone and a faster start.

## Shared playlists

- **One playlist, several people**: share a playlist from its menu with a code or a link. Everyone with a Ketvoice account can add, remove and reorder songs, and all see the changes within minutes.
- Shared playlists carry a *Shared* badge in your library, play like any other, and have a members sheet with everyone's photo. The owner can remove someone; anyone can leave.
- Join with a code from the library, or by opening an invitation link.

## Public profile

- Choose to make your profile public: your name, your photo, the playlists you publish and, only if you turn it on, the song you are listening to now. Both switches are off until you turn them on, in your Ketvoice account.
- Open a profile by its handle, from a link or from the search in your account.

## Air Ket

- **Move everything to a new phone, directly**: on the old phone open *Air Ket* in Settings, on the new one choose *Receive with Air Ket*, and type the six-digit code (or scan the QR code with the camera). No account and no server: the two phones talk over your Wi-Fi, encrypted.
- It carries your settings, favourites, playlists, followed and blocked artists, history, Replay and custom lyrics, and, if you want, your downloaded songs with their lyrics, covers and Canvas.
- You choose to replace or merge what the new phone already has, and a transfer that stops picks up where it left off.

## Canvas

- **Kept on your phone**: a Canvas you imported from a link, like every other one, is kept once it has loaded, so it opens at once, online or not, and goes with a downloaded song. One Canvas is shared by all the songs of an album unless a song has its own.
- **Ahead of time**: the Canvas of the next songs are fetched while the current one plays, and, on Wi-Fi, those of your favourites and playlists.
- **In Settings**: see how much space Canvas use, choose the limit (256 MB to 2 GB) and clear them. The ones of your downloads are never removed. A small *Canvas…* pill shows while one is loading.

## New releases

- A notification when an artist you follow puts out an album, a single or an EP (Settings, on by default). Tap it to open the artist.

## Faster

- Ketvoice opens faster: its settings are read beside the screen being built, and what the first screen does not need starts right after it.

## Vice City

- The Vice City theme now has its striped setting sun behind the greeting, and the player's seek bar and the sliders fill with the sunset.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.3.0.apk` (phone), `Ketvoice-6.3.0-Lite.apk` (lighter phone app) or `Ketvoice-6.3.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
