# Ketvoice 3.0.3 — adaptive artist banners

**Mobile/tablet and Quest · versionCode 28**

- Artist pages now use the measured aspect ratio of the selected catalog image.
  A short panoramic image produces a shorter header instead of a large empty
  block of flat color before the artist name.
- The photo-to-color fade begins before the image ends. Portrait artwork, when
  provided by the catalog, is preferred for the taller immersive layout.
- The biography, playback and mini-player behavior remain unchanged.

## Validation and limits

- Both flavor JVM suites, release lint, signed APK verification and hashes are
  recorded in the accompanying `validation.json` and `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- Juice WRLD's public YouTube Music header was observed to supply only a short
  landscape banner (1000 × 416 at the largest returned size). A taller portrait
  cannot be reconstructed without cropping or a different image source.
- No connected phone or Quest was available in this session. Installation,
  actual UI rendering and the live artist page on a device were not tested.
  No emulator was installed or used, as requested.
