# Ketvoice 2.6.0

**Android 12+ · versionCode 13 · 22 October 2026**

## What's new

- **Tablet-ready Liquid Glass:** the real window size decides the layout. A compact labelled rail appears at tablet widths, an expanded rail on wide landscapes, track grids gain columns, Explore tiles adapt, and Settings/import forms keep readable widths.
- **Two-pane player:** artwork and controls sit beside synchronized lyrics or the queue when the window has room. A short landscape window, such as a phone, keeps the single-pane landscape player instead of squeezing two panes.
- **Artist searches with songs:** searching an artist name now shows that artist's songs in a separate section as well as the artist card. Song requests use the same `WEB_REMIX` client as artist/album lookups, while still requiring each row's own official-audio watch endpoint.
- **Keyboard-safe search:** floating navigation and the mini-player hide while the on-screen keyboard is open, so results are no longer covered on phones.
- **Public publications:** the verified **konnektsupport@gmail.com** mailbox can import songs and lyrics from the app. Published songs appear in normal search and the Ketvoice API source, with public artwork, streaming, downloads, and lyrics.
- **Supplementary public lyrics:** imported TTML/LRC lyrics are published as a new public version and add a selectable **Ketvoice API** provider. Auto mode may use it when it answers within a short window, then falls back to the existing providers. Timing is preserved, never invented.
- **Server-enforced publishing:** a matching YouTube profile only reveals the entry point. The `ketvoice-content` broker re-checks the Supabase Auth user and its confirmed email on every write, rejects forged/unauthenticated requests, and performs validated uploads with a server-side service role. No client write grants exist on the public tables or media bucket.

## Publishing (authorized account only)

1. Connect the authorized YouTube account and open **Settings → Publications Ketvoice API**.
2. Request a one-time Supabase email login link, then copy the received link into the app without opening/consuming it, or enter a code if the mail template provides one.
3. **Import a song:** choose MP3, M4A, AAC, FLAC, WAV, OGG, or Opus (max 25 MiB), optionally add a JPEG/PNG/WebP cover (max 4 MiB) and TTML/LRC lyrics (max 1 MiB).
4. **Import lyrics:** search normally, open a track's ⋯ menu, choose **Import lyrics**, and preview the native timing before publishing a supplementary public version.
5. Confirm that you own the necessary rights and that the files may be distributed publicly.

Refresh tokens are encrypted with Android Keystore. The YouTube session cookie is never sent to the publication service. Published audio, covers, lyrics, and metadata are public to every user and consume the project's storage/egress quotas.

## Limitations

- A real authenticated upload requires the mailbox owner to complete the Supabase email login. Development did not impersonate that account or send mail automatically.
- Email delivery depends on the project's Supabase Auth templates and rate limits. Some templates deliver a magic link without a numeric code; in that case, copy the link into the app without opening it.
- Published media is public and counts against the Supabase project's storage/egress quotas. There is no separate moderation queue beyond the single authorized publisher.
- Tablet validation is emulator-only; no physical tablet or device farm is available.
- Third-party services may rate-limit requests or lack the selected audio quality, lyrics, or Canvas. A download may fall back to a lower audio quality; the app reports the format actually saved.
- Only the final 2.6.0 APK is attached. Historical credential-bearing APKs (1.2.0–2.3.0) remain withheld.
