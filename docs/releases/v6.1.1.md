# Ketvoice 6.1.1

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A safety net for a crash at launch.

## Fixes

- **Safe mode**: if Ketvoice closes twice in a row while starting, the next launch opens a simple screen instead, with the crash report and buttons to share it, copy it or try again. Send that report to fix the cause quickly.
- Crash reports are now kept from the very start of the app, including the playback service.

Everything in [6.1.0](v6.1.0.md) is included: Ketogether, the Ketmix editor, lock-screen lyrics, weekly discoveries and Replay, Ketvoice Lite and the Vice City edition.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.1.1.apk` (phone), `Ketvoice-6.1.1-Lite.apk` (lighter phone app) or `Ketvoice-6.1.1-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
