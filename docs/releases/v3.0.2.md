# Ketvoice 3.0.2 — artist image framing

**Mobile/tablet and Quest · versionCode 27**

- The artist page now prefers wide header artwork when YouTube Music provides
  it. Square portraits display at the available screen width rather than being
  enlarged to cover the entire tall header.
- The hero grows with the viewport, fading from the photo into a softer tone
  sampled from it. The name and functional shuffle, play and share controls
  sit below the image, inspired by the supplied Werenoi reference.
- The biography, tracks and albums remain below the hero. Player navigation
  and background audio from 3.0.1 are unchanged.

## Build validation and limits

- Mobile and Quest JVM tests, release lint and signed APK verification are
  recorded in the accompanying `validation.json` and `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- No phone or physical Quest was connected in this session. Device installation,
  live artist artwork and on-device visual comparison remain for the user's
  phone check. No emulator tests are claimed or performed.
