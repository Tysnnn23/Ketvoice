# Ketvoice 6.6.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A smoother app, Playlists and Downloads redone in the style of Apple Music, better layouts on tablets, and a quieter Vice City theme.

## Smoother

- Home, the Library, Wrapped and loading screens no longer redraw themselves several times a second while music plays.
- The Albums and Artists grids build only the rows you see, and searching the Library is faster.
- Background downloads no longer make every cover on the page redraw.
- Pictures are decoded at the size of your screen, never larger, so scrolling stays even.
- On phones with less memory, Ketvoice now uses the lighter settings of Ketvoice Lite that you cannot see.

## Playlists

- Library › Playlists is a clean list or grid of covers with « Playlist · owner » under each name, and « New playlist » and « Import playlists » at the top.
- The menu at the top right switches between list and grid, sorts by date, title or number of songs, and filters pinned or shared playlists.
- A playlist page shows the large cover, the owner in your accent colour, Play and Shuffle side by side, the songs, and the total « N songs, X h Y min » at the bottom.
- « Edit order » is in the playlist's menu. Share, Members and « Make this playlist public » now work from the playlist page too.

## Downloads

- A large title with the number of songs, their size and length, Play and Shuffle, and a simple storage card.
- Each song shows its format, size, lyrics, Canvas and separated-voice badges; removing a download is in its « … » menu.

## Tablets

- The sidebar stays as it is. Cover grids now fit the window: 4 columns in portrait, 6 in landscape.
- Playlist and album pages put the cover beside the title, with the songs full width below.
- Lists go two by two on wide windows.

## Vice City

- The neon is gone. The theme keeps your light or dark look and adds a flamingo-pink accent and one flat sunset with palm trees, on the Vice FM card and the welcome screen.
- The theme is still available until 10 December.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.6.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-6.6.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-6.6.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
