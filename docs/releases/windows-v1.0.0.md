# Ketvoice for Windows 1.0.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

The first version of Ketvoice for Windows. It is a native app with the same library, Ketmix, lyrics and account as the phone, laid out for a computer: a sidebar, a player bar at the top and pages you can resize.

## Listening

- Streams and downloads play through FFmpeg, with gapless playback, a 10-band equalizer and a Lossless or Hi-Res badge when the file really is one.
- Downloads work offline, show their progress live and can be upgraded to a better quality, one song at a time.
- Local music: choose folders on your computer and their songs appear under Songs, Albums and Artists.
- The Windows media controls, the media keys and the notification area all drive the player. Closing the window can leave Ketvoice playing in the notification area.
- A mini player that stays on top of other windows (Ctrl+M).

## Ketmix

- Songs mix into each other on two decks, planned by the same analysis as on the phone: beat matching with the pitch kept, a bass swap, filters, echo, and a gentle return to the original tempo afterwards.
- Skipping a song can mix it instead of cutting it.
- Optional voice separation: cut or keep the vocals in a transition, and a voice level slider beside the lyrics, like on the phone.
- The optional rhythm and voice models download once and are checked before use.

## Lyrics and Canvas

- Synced lyrics word by word, with duets on each side, translation, and a romanization switch at the top of the lyrics sources. Click a line to jump to it.
- Animated Canvas videos in the full player and as a motion header at the top of album pages, kept on your computer once played.
- The full player's background moves with the colours of the artwork.

## Your account

- Sign in with email or Discord, and your favourites, playlists and settings follow you between the phone, the web and the computer.
- Control your phone from the computer and the computer from your phone: see what plays, change songs, the position and the volume, or move playback from one to the other.
- A live Statistics page: your minutes, top songs and artists, and when you listen.
- Import playlists from Apple Music, Tidal, SoundCloud, Spotify and Deezer links.

## Everywhere

- Search ranked like on the phone, complete artist and album pages, and “Play next” and “Add to playlist” in every menu.
- Keyboard shortcuts: space to play or pause, arrows to seek and change the volume, Ctrl+F to search, and more, listed in Settings.
- All 11 interface languages, switched without a restart.
- Updates: Ketvoice checks for new Windows versions, verifies the download and installs it over the current version.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-1.0.0.msi` below and open it. It works on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown: choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. To remove Ketvoice, use Installed apps in Windows Settings; your library stays in your account.
