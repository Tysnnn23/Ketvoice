# Ketvoice 5.11.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketmix gets ready from the first seconds of a song and mixes smarter; Ket 27 moves like iOS.

## Ketmix

- **Ready from the first seconds.** As soon as a song has played a few seconds, Ketmix reads, measures and separates its ending and the songs that follow — three ahead on Wi-Fi, one on mobile data. The mix is ready long before the end, even if you jump forward in the song, and everything is kept for the next time those songs play.
- **A real mix when you skip.** Press next while Ketmix is on: when the next song is prepared and loads at once, it is mixed in over a few seconds on two decks, entering where its music starts, instead of a plain fade.
- **Downloads prepared while charging** (Settings → Ketmix → *Prepare downloaded songs*): with the phone plugged in, downloaded songs are analysed and their voices separated before they are ever played.
- **Knows where the singing is.** The voice separation now also tells Ketmix where each song starts and stops singing, so songs without lyrics are no longer mixed over their last sung words, and a long instrumental ending is left earlier.
- **A voice over the other song's instrumental.** When two songs share tempo and key and the next one sings early, its voice alone comes in over the current song's instrumental, then the whole song takes over as the bass changes hands. Needs the separation model.
- **Smart queue** (Settings → Ketmix, on by default): in shuffle, the song that plays next is the one, among the next few already measured, that goes best with the current one in tempo and key. A queue you ordered yourself and a song you asked to play next never move.

## Ket 27

- **The mini-player unfolds into the player.** One spring opens and closes it: the title, the artist, play and next travel from the mini-player to their place, and a flick down closes the player from anywhere, at the speed of your finger.
- **Icons that move.** Play and pause melt into each other, the heart fills and bounces, the download arrow fills as the song arrives and its check draws itself, shuffle and repeat pulse when you toggle them.
- **Context menus and spring sheets.** Hold a song: it lifts and a glass menu unfolds from it (play next, queue, favourite, download, playlist, radio, share). Sheets rest at half height or full height, follow the finger and settle with a spring.
- **Controls with a click.** Sliders get a glass thumb and a tick at each step (one per dB on the equalizer), the volume rail swells under the finger, switches and pickers tick, pickers become inset lists.
- **More pages in the Ket 27 look**: large titles on Home, Explorer, Search, Stats; glass back button and centred titles on pushed pages; rounded glass alerts; menus that take the page's colours. Ket 27 stays an Alpha: tablet and Quest players and a few dialogs keep their previous look.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-5.11.0.apk` (phone) or `Ketvoice-5.11.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
