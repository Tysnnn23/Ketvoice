# Ketvoice for Windows 2.0.2

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Send your music to Ketvoice TV from your computer.

## Play on the TV

- When Ketvoice TV is signed in with your account, it appears in the Devices panel. Choose "Play on TV" and the song moves to the TV, which starts playing at once. The computer pauses.
- Only your own devices can receive your music, and a request that arrives more than 30 seconds late is ignored.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-2.0.2.msi` below and open it. It installs over any earlier Ketvoice for Windows and keeps your library, settings and downloads. It works on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown: choose "More info", then "Run anyway". If you already use Ketvoice for Windows, it can also offer the update itself.
