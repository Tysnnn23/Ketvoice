# Ketvoice 9.1.1

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketmix mixes for real instead of falling back to an echo, and your listening now follows you between your phone and your computer.

## Ketmix

- **Far fewer echoes**: Ketmix now blends songs together most of the time. An echo only comes when it clearly suits, such as a song that stops on a sung line before a very different tempo, and never twice in a row. If you pick the Echo style yourself, you still get it.
- **No more abrupt changes after a hard ending**: when a song stops at full volume, Ketmix mixes out a little earlier, at the start of the last phrase, so the next song is blended in before the final hit.
- **Smoother transitions**: a plain fade no longer swells when the next song starts during the end of the current one, the bass changes hands faster on the beat, and echoes no longer click.
- **Your chosen styles play again**: Echo and Rise, when picked by hand, now play even when the two tempos are close.

## Everything in sync

- **Recently played and Statistics now sync** with your account: your phone and Ketvoice for Windows show the same recent songs, minutes, top songs and artists. Each play is counted once, by the device that played it.
- **Discord**: your "show what I'm listening to" choice follows you, and each device shows where Discord is connected.
- Favourites, playlists, artists and settings keep syncing as before.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-9.1.1.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-9.1.1-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-9.1.1-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
