# Ketvoice 6.4.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Follow your friends, brand-new widgets, an offline library that fills itself, your own music cleaned up — and a neon night for Vice City.

## Friends

- **Follow public profiles** and see their activity on Home: the playlists they publish, the songs they add and, if they share it, what they are listening to. React with an emoji; tap a line to play it.
- Your followers, the people you follow and the profiles you blocked are in your Ketvoice account, with *Publish my activity* to stop sharing at any time. Only public profiles can be followed, and activity older than 30 days disappears.

## Widgets, redone

- Every widget follows your launcher's rounded corners, takes the colours of the cover, adapts to any size and looks right in light and dark.
- New widgets: **Live lyrics** (the sung line and the next one), **Ketmix** (a switch and what it mixes next) and **Replay** (this month's minutes and your top artist and song).
- **Quick Settings tiles** for play/pause and Ketmix, and *Add the widget* buttons in Settings → Appearance → Widgets.

## Smart offline

- Turn it on in Settings → Downloads: on Wi-Fi (and while charging, if you like), Ketvoice downloads your new favourites, what you play most, your most played playlists and the next songs of your weekly discoveries — within the space you choose (1 to 10 GB, or a share of the free space).
- To make room it only removes what it downloaded itself, least played first; it never touches your own downloads and never brings back a song you deleted. Its downloads carry an *Auto* badge.

## Your own music

- Tags read and cleaned, corrections proposed from the catalogue (accept them per album or all at once), covers from the file, the folder or the catalogue, and synced lyrics found for you.
- Songs, Albums (with discs and track numbers), Artists and Folders views, and *Also on streaming* under the songs the catalogue has too.
- Ketvoice writes into your files only if you choose *Write to the file* (MP3 and FLAC), and keeps a copy of the original while it writes.

## Vice City, neon night

- A synthwave floor scrolls under the tab bar, the large titles shine in 80s chrome with a neon underline, and the KETVOICE sign flickers on when Home opens.
- The player gets a neon ring on play/pause and pink and cyan times; a held note in the lyrics glows from pink to cyan; menus, sheets and messages get a neon edge.
- **Vice FM** on Home: a fresh mix of synthwave and 80s hits from the catalogue at every tap.
- The theme stays available until 10 December. Ketvoice is not affiliated with Rockstar Games.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.4.0.apk` (phone), `Ketvoice-6.4.0-Lite.apk` (lighter phone app) or `Ketvoice-6.4.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
