# Ketvoice 5.5.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketmix plays far more titles in time, and Ket 27 (Alpha) gives Ketvoice the interface of iOS 27.

## Ketmix (beta)

- **A tempo read where the drums play.** Ketmix now also listens to 30 seconds from the body of each title and reads its intro and its ending against that tempo. An intro without drums, or an ending where only hats in triplets are left, could be counted at 3/2 or 2/3 of the real tempo — or not counted at all — which left most pairs to a filter crossing or an echo. With the balanced and maximum analysis settings they now get the title's own beat grid.
- **In time across wider tempo gaps.** Two titles are now played in time when their tempos are within 6 % (discreet), 14 % (balanced) or 18 % (DJ), instead of 5, 8 and 12 %. The common tempo slides from the outgoing title's to the incoming one's during the blend, so each title is only stretched while it is the quiet one, with its pitch kept. A pair such as 138 into 122 BPM is now a blend on the beat with the bass changing hands, not a crossing of filters.
- **The mix journal** also shows the tempo read from the body of each title.

## Ket 27 (Alpha)

A new switch in Settings → Appearance turns the interface towards iOS 27. It is an alpha: the pages below are done, others still keep the previous style.

- **System colours.** The grouped background and white cells of iOS in light mode, pure black and dark grey cells in dark mode, and the label and separator colours of the system.
- **Typography.** Inter, the open typeface closest to San Francisco, with the iOS text styles: 34 pt bold large titles, 17 pt body, 13 pt footnotes.
- **Settings as an inset grouped list.** 26 pt rounded groups, symbols on coloured tiles, headers in sentence case, a large title on the root page and a uniform bar with the page's title once inside a category.
- **Controls.** The 63 × 28 switch of iOS 27, green when on, with a wide capsule thumb; alerts with 34 pt corners.
- **Tab bar.** 62 pt high, the selected tab tinted with your accent colour.
- **Glass.** Liquid 27 is used everywhere while Ket 27 is on.
