# Ketvoice 4.17.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Smoother lyrics, a navigation bar that comes back when you need it, and glass that
catches the light.

## Lyrics

- **Share a moment works again.** After a long-press on lyrics, the Share button now
  sits beside Cancel; before, Cancel took the whole row and hid it.
- **Sung lines stay white.** In the Ketvoice and Cascade styles, lines already sung keep
  full brightness; only the lines still to come are dimmed.
- **A smoother Cascade.** The lines below glide into place one after another on a soft
  ease-out, without the jump and overshoot of before.

## Navigation

- **The bar comes back on a small scroll up.** On Home, Explore and Library, the floating
  bar and mini-player fold when you scroll down and unfold as soon as you scroll back up a
  little, wherever you are on the page.
- **Pages push like on iOS.** A new page slides in from the edge on a spring while the
  page underneath recedes and dims; going back reverses it.

## Look

- **Liquid Glass catches the light.** Buttons and bars gain a broad, soft highlight on
  the bevel facing the light and a light Fresnel edge, closer to the glass of iOS.
- **A tighter album page.** Title, artist and details sit closer together, and track
  rows are more compact, so more of the album fits on screen.
- **Artist signatures.** With artist signatures enabled in Settings, an artist without
  an authenticated signature can now show one from the public signature archive
  (Wikimedia Commons and a CC0 archive), matched to the exact artist name.
