# Ketvoice 8.2.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A player background that moves with the colours of the artwork, livelier lyrics, featured cards on Home, and Ketvoice that adapts itself to your battery, your phone's heat and your Wi-Fi.

## Look

- **A living player background**: the colours of the artwork now flow slowly behind the player, like a soft gradient, instead of a fixed blurred picture. It rests when the Canvas plays, with reduced motion and in performance mode.
- **Livelier lyrics**: lines settle into place on springs, the word being sung grows very slightly, and the lines already sung fade softly. Duets keep their sides, and a held note still glows a little.
- **Featured on Home**: large cards at the top of Home. The card in the middle plays its Canvas silently; the others wait, so the row stays light.

## Faster and lighter

- The startup profile now covers the parts of the app used most (opening the app, Home, the player, lists and playback), so the phone prepares them when it installs Ketvoice.
- On Wi-Fi, Ketvoice gets ready for what you are likely to open next: the album and artist of the song playing, the covers of the next songs and of the first Home rows.
- **Automatic power saving**: when the battery is at 20 % or less without charging, or when the phone gets hot, Ketvoice switches to performance mode on its own and tells you, then switches back. You can turn this off in Settings.

## Diagnostic

- A new “Diagnostic” switch in Settings, under About, records how the app runs on your phone: dropped frames, start time, time to the first sound, how long Home, search and pages take, and memory. “Share the diagnostic” gives you the file to send if you report a slowdown. It is off by default, nothing is ever sent on its own, and the file holds no song titles, searches or account details.

## Ketmix

- Your Ketmix Studio presets now follow your Ketvoice account, so the presets you save on your phone are also on your other devices, including Ketvoice for Windows.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-8.2.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-8.2.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-8.2.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
