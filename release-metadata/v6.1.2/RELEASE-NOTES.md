# Ketvoice 6.1.2

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketvoice opens again.

## Fixes

- **No more crash at launch**: since 6.1.0 the app closed as soon as it opened on some phones — Android refused to load part of Ketmix because one of its steps had grown too large. That step is now split up, and every build checks that no part of the app can grow past that limit again.
- The safe mode of 6.1.1 stays: if Ketvoice ever closes twice in a row while starting, a simple screen shows the crash report to share.

Everything in [6.1.0](v6.1.0.md) is included: Ketogether, the Ketmix editor, lock-screen lyrics, weekly discoveries and Replay, Ketvoice Lite and the Vice City edition.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.1.2.apk` (phone), `Ketvoice-6.1.2-Lite.apk` (lighter phone app) or `Ketvoice-6.1.2-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
