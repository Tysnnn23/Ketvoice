# Ketvoice 5.7.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketmix without speed effects and with a new build-up transition, translated biographies, artists you can block, and the tab bar lens of iOS.

## Ketmix (beta)

- **No more speed effects.** A title's speed never changes any more while it is heard. Before, titles at different tempos were sped up or slowed down during and after a blend, and each change could be heard as a small cut. Now two titles are only played in time when their tempos are within 2 % (discreet), 3 % (balanced) or 4 % (DJ) of each other: the next title is given that speed once, while it is still silent, keeps it to its end, and the title after it starts on its own tempo again. Titles further apart leave on a build-up, an echo or a crossing of filters.
- **Build-up, a new transition.** Over the last bars of a phrase the title thins out through a rising filter while a breath of noise swells, then the next title drops in on its first beat — no common tempo needed. Left to Ketmix, a build-up and an echo take turns; it can also be chosen as the transition style (*Build*).
- Corrections that keep two titles in time are only made while the next title cannot be heard yet, early enough to be over before it is.
- The mix journal shows the real speed the next title plays at.

## Artists

- **Block or keep an artist.** In the search, a long press on an artist's circle opens a small menu: *Block artist*, *Unblock artist*, *Add to favourites*. A blocked artist is left out of what Ketvoice proposes — Home, its mixes, the titles a search finds in the catalogue, the titles added when the queue runs out — wherever they are credited, features included; your playlists, downloads and library are not touched. Your favourite artists appear at the top of the search.
- **Biographies in your language.** An artist's biography is translated into the language of the app, with *Show original* one tap away.

## Home

- **The For you card changes.** New releases and your mixes take turns on the large card at the top of Home: the one shown the least comes first, never the same twice in a row.

## Liquid Glass

- **The tab bar lens.** Hold the selection of the tab bar, or slide along it, and it becomes a lens as on iOS: it swells, turns clear, magnifies the tab under it and follows your finger, with a tick for each tab it crosses; let go and it settles on that tab.
- With Ket 27, the thumb of a switch also becomes a clear magnifying lens while it is held.

## Privacy

- To translate a biography, its text (public catalogue text) is sent to Google Translate; the translation is kept on the device. See PRIVACY.md.
