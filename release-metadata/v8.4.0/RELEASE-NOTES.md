# Ketvoice 8.4.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A new glass look called Crystal, Ketvoice in the car the way you would expect it, a Ketmix that bridges any two tempos, and a set of everyday helpers: the audio output you want, a search in Settings, playlists that stay up to date and a year recap with its own soundtrack.

## Crystal

- **A second glass look**: pick “Glass style” in the Glass settings and choose Crystal. The tab bar, the mini player, the selection pill and the menus take a new clear glass, with its own springs, a pill that lifts, stretches and lands, and menus that bloom from the button. Liquid 27 stays the default and is unchanged.

## In the car

- **Voice**: ask Google to play an artist, a song, an album, a genre or one of your playlists on Ketvoice, to play your likes or your downloads, to start a radio from an artist or a song, or just to play some music to pick up where you left off.
- **A real library on the car screen**: Home with your mixes and shelves, Library (playlists, albums, artists, songs, likes and downloads), Radio and Downloads, with covers, “Play all” and “Shuffle” at the top of every list.
- **The whole queue** shows on the car screen, and a tap jumps to any song in it.
- **Buttons on the playing screen**: like, shuffle, repeat, a radio from the current song and Ketmix on or off. They also appear in the phone's media controls.
- Downloads stay browsable and playable without a connection, and covers you have seen once keep showing offline.

## Ketmix

- **Loop bridge**: a new transition for two songs far apart in tempo. The outgoing song hands over to a four-bar instrumental loop, both songs glide together to the new tempo while staying on the beat, and the next song comes in on its own instrumental bars. Ketmix picks it by itself when the tempos are too far apart for a normal blend, and you can choose it in Ketmix Studio.
- **Tighter beats**: beats are now placed between the analysis frames instead of on a 20 ms grid, so blends land closer to the beat.

## Everyday

- **Audio output**: the audio output sheet now lists your real outputs (Bluetooth, wired, USB, the phone's speaker, hearing aids) and sends Ketvoice's sound to the one you pick, live as devices come and go.
- **Search in Settings**: type at the top of Settings to find any option, with or without accents; a tap opens it and highlights it.
- **Downloaded playlists stay up to date**: when a playlist you downloaded gets new songs, a notification offers to download just those. You can turn it off in the Downloads settings.
- **A soundtrack for your year recap**: each card of the recap plays the best part of its song and fades into the next one. Your own queue, history and statistics are left untouched, and a button mutes it.
- **Hosting a Ketogether session**: set how many people can join (2 to 10), remove a listener, see who stepped away with their place kept, follow a short log of who paused, skipped or added a song, and check how close everyone is in sync.
- **Lyrics source order**: put the lyrics sources in the order you prefer in the lyrics settings; the first one that has the lyrics wins.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-8.4.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-8.4.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-8.4.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
