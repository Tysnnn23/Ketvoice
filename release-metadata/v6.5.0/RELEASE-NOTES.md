# Ketvoice 6.5.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketvoice Lite now runs on Android 11 and is tuned for phones and tablets with little memory — with the same effects.

## Ketvoice Lite

- **Android 11 and up**: Ketvoice Lite now installs on Android 11. The glass keeps its frosted blur there too — Ketvoice computes it itself where Android 11 cannot — and the blurred cover backgrounds and the rounded widgets look the same as on newer phones.
- **Phones and tablets with little memory**: on a device with 2 to 4 GB, Lite keeps every effect you see and makes each one lighter to draw — blur and glow worked out at a lower resolution, images decoded no larger than the screen, one Canvas video at a time, voice separation with the quick model away from the charger, and memory freed as soon as Android asks.
- **Tablets**: Lite gets the same large-screen layouts as the regular app — the glass sidebar, two panes and the side-by-side player.
- **Settings → About** shows which mode is active and why, for example « This phone has 2 GB of memory: lighter effects, identical to the eye ».

## Also

- The glass in Blur mode no longer washes out to white on some phones.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.5.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-6.5.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-6.5.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
