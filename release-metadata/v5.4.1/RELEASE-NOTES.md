# Ketvoice 5.4.1

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketmix transitions that keep their effects, last notes that ring on, and intros that rise under the last words.

## Ketmix (beta)

- **Effects instead of a plain fade.** Ketmix now gives both players a moment to have their audio processing ready before it chooses a transition, and keeps the next title silent until its effects are live. Filtered, layered and echoed transitions are played where a plain volume fade was often used instead.
- **A last note rings on.** A title that ends on a last note at full level is no longer simply joined to the next one: the next title still starts right after that note, on the beat when there is one, and the last beat rings on in an echo for four beats, played out past the end of the title. The echo takes its length from the tempo even when the tempo was not read with enough confidence to mix in time. The discreet setting keeps the plain join.
- **Intros rise under the last words.** When the next title does not sing for a while, it now comes up under the last sung words of the outgoing one — for up to 6 seconds (balanced) or 10 seconds (DJ) — while the outgoing voice steps back, instead of being crossed in 2.5 seconds once the singing is over. Its own voice still arrives after the crossing.
- **A more detailed mix journal.** Each transition now also shows where the outgoing title's sound ends and how (hard, fading or ringing), where the mix started in both titles, whether it rang on past the end, and whether both players' effects were in use.
