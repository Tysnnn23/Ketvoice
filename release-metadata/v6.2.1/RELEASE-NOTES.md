# Ketvoice 6.2.1

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

The Vice City theme gets its sunset.

## Vice City

- **The setting sun**: Home now has the striped sun of the 80s posters behind the greeting, fading from orange to pink and slowly breathing with the glow.
- **A sunset on every slider**: the seek bar of the player and the sliders across the app fill with the Vice City sunset, with a soft glow under the fill while you hold them.
- The theme stays available until 10 December.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.2.1.apk` (phone), `Ketvoice-6.2.1-Lite.apk` (lighter phone app) or `Ketvoice-6.2.1-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
