# Ketvoice 6.0.0 Beta 5

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Ketvoice in the car with Android Auto, music that keeps playing in the background, sliders that swell under your finger like iOS 27, and Ketmix's sign under the seek bar.

## Android Auto

- **Browse from the car**: Home (your mixes and recently played), Favourites, Playlists and Downloads, with covers, in four tabs.
- **Search**: your playlists, your library and the whole catalogue, from the car's search screen.
- **Voice**: "Hey Google, play … on Ketvoice" plays the best match first and the other results after it; a playlist's name plays that playlist, and "my favourites" shuffles them.
- Pressing play in the car picks up where you left off, or starts your favourites.
- A song chosen in the car plays exactly as on the phone: downloaded copy first, then the best stream, with the whole list queued for next and previous.

## Background playback

- **Three minutes read ahead** instead of one: a phone that cuts the network of apps in the background has to hold it off far longer before the music stops, and the radio wakes less often.
- Away from the screen and on battery, Ketmix and voice separation do only the essential, and the separation models leave memory: battery managers stop the biggest apps first.
- **Honor and Huawei phones**: Settings opens the *App launch* screen directly, with the steps that keep the music going (turn off *Manage automatically*, then turn on *Run in background*).
- The playback journal (Settings → Playback) now also notes waits for data, low memory and data saver, to find the cause of a cut at once.

## Ket 27

- **Sliders like iOS 27**: hold the seek bar, the volume, the voice slider or any setting slider and it swells and reaches out under your finger; pull past an end and it stretches like rubber, then springs back.
- **Ketmix's sign** between the times under the seek bar while two songs are mixed: two rings that merge at the hand-over.
- **The player** is back to its artwork with the controls below it; the full-screen glass player is gone.
- **Lyrics**: the glow of a sung word is much fainter, and none lingers at the end of a line.
- Rows of songs light up briefly under a touch, empty pages have a symbol and a clearer layout, and lists show placeholder rows while they load.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.0.0-beta.5.apk` (phone) or `Ketvoice-6.0.0-beta.5-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay. Android Auto lists an app installed outside the Play Store once *Unknown sources* is turned on in Android Auto's developer settings.
