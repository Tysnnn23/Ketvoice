# Ketvoice 8.1.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A faster Ketvoice: smoother scrolling and player animations, a Home that comes up at once, quicker searches and pages, the Canvas back edge to edge, and control of Ketvoice for Windows from your phone.

## Smoother

- Scrolling Home with the tab bar folding, and opening or closing the full player, now redraw far less of the screen at every frame. The glass, the fold and the player animation look the same.
- Phone control reads its settings away from the screen, so opening the player never waits on it.

## Faster

- The Home sections from your YouTube Music account appear at once from the copy kept last time, then refresh. “New from your artists” loads about three times faster.
- Playing a song again, or going back to the previous one, starts much sooner.
- Searching the same thing twice answers immediately, and searches no longer ask the same source twice.
- Album and artist pages start loading as soon as you touch them.
- The Canvas of the song on screen comes before the ones prepared ahead, so it shows about twice as fast.
- Ketvoice opens with less work on the screen's thread.

## Player

- The Canvas fills the top of the player and the album header edge to edge again, as before 7.6, instead of being shown whole with stretched colours around it.
- In the lyrics or the queue, a tap on the small cover in the header brings you back to the player.

## Your devices

- A new “Your devices” section in the audio output sheet shows Ketvoice running on your other devices, including Ketvoice for Windows. See what plays there, change songs, the position and the volume, or move playback from one device to the other.
- It works between devices signed in to the same Ketvoice account, through a private key that only your devices know.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-8.1.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-8.1.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-8.1.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay. Ketvoice for Windows is on the [Windows release](https://github.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/releases/tag/windows-v1.0.0).
