# Ketvoice 7.5.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Google Cast, lyrics you can pronounce, album pages with truer colours, artwork that stays cached, faster downloads, one glass slider and a standby screen for your nightstand.

## Listen anywhere

- **Google Cast** (phone and Lite): send your music to a Google or Nest speaker, a Chromecast TV or a sound bar from « Sortie audio ». Downloads can be cast too. Ketmix, voice level and the equalizer pause while casting.
- **Romanisation**: a new switch at the top of the lyrics source menu shows how each line is pronounced in Latin letters, under the lyrics. It covers Japanese kana, Chinese (pinyin), Korean, Russian, Ukrainian, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi and Thai.
- **Standby mode**: while your phone charges in landscape, Ketvoice can show a large clock, the cover or Canvas, the title and the line being sung. Turn it on in Settings; Ketvoice is also offered as a system screensaver.

## Album pages

- **Truer colours**: album pages pick the colour you see on the cover, not a small detail of it. For example, The Party Never Ends is now purple, 1989 blue and black covers stay black. The player and widgets use the same colours.
- **Canvas animé**: choose it in Settings and an album with a Canvas opens like Apple Music, with the video across the whole top of the screen and the title, artist, Play and Shuffle over its fade. The former « Cover and Canvas » choice is replaced by it.
- Canvas appear at once: a still of the first frame shows while the video starts, the album keeps its Canvas when you come back, and album Canvas are fetched ahead on Wi-Fi.
- The cover is bigger, the title, artist and buttons sit closer together, and the header scrolls away with the list instead of fading out.

## Faster

- **Artwork and Canvas stay cached**: an album's Canvas shows at once the second time, without waiting for the network, and covers you have seen appear immediately.
- **Faster downloads**: large files download over several connections, and the check for a better quality stops as soon as a lossless version is found.
- **Faster Ketmix analysis**: the slowest steps of preparing a song for Ketmix are several times faster.
- **Improve one song**: « Améliorer la qualité » is now in the menu of each downloaded song, no selection needed.

## Playback

- Pausing and resuming fade in a fraction of a second instead of cutting.
- **Mix when I skip**: a new switch in Settings → Ketmix. Turn it off and Next changes song right away, even with Ketmix on.

## Glass and lists

- **One glass slider**: a single slider in Appearance goes from transparent to tinted, replacing the Clear/Tinted switch and the old transparency steps.
- **Controls that turn to glass**: while you hold the seek bar, the volume or the voice slider, a small glass lens forms under your finger.
- **Search and sort in your lists**: playlists, Favourites and Downloads have a search field and sorting by date added, title, artist, album or duration.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-7.5.0.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-7.5.0-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-7.5.0-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
