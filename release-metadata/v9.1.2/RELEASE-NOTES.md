# Ketvoice 9.1.2

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

Send your music to Ketvoice TV in one tap.

## Play on the TV

- When Ketvoice TV is signed in with your account, it appears in the devices list of the player with a TV icon. Choose "Play on TV" and the song moves to the TV, which starts playing at once. The phone pauses.
- Only your own devices can receive your music, and a request that arrives more than 30 seconds late is ignored, so a TV that was asleep never starts playing on its own later.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-9.1.2.apk` (phone, Android 12 and up), `Ketvoice-9.1.2-Lite.apk` (lighter app, Android 11 and up) or `Ketvoice-9.1.2-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay.
