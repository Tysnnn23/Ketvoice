# Ketvoice 5.8.0

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

A faster app, music that keeps playing behind other apps, search by lyrics, radios, and the Apple touch on the library, pages, icons and animations.

## Faster

- **The app is now built with Android's optimiser (R8)**, which streamlines the libraries the interface is made of. Screens, scrolling and animations respond faster, with every effect kept.
- Behind other apps, Ketvoice leaves the processor to the music: the reverb no longer works sample by sample while it is off, the position of the title is read once a second instead of five times, Canvas videos wait until the app is back on screen, and Ketmix only uses what it already measured.

## Music without cuts in the background

- Many phones put a music app to sleep to save battery, which is heard as cuts. The first time music plays on such a phone, Ketvoice offers to let Android play it **without battery restrictions**; the choice is also in Settings → Playback → *Background playback*.

## New

- **Search by lyrics.** Type a few words of a song: titles whose lyrics are already on your phone are found at once, and for a sentence of three words or more the search also asks Genius, which knows the lyrics of titles you have never played.
- **Radio.** *Start radio* in a title's menu, or *Artist radio* on an artist page: a queue that never runs out, alternating the artist and artists like them, never twice the same title and never an artist you blocked.
- **Favourites offline automatically.** A title you make a favourite downloads by itself (Settings → Downloads; the Wi-Fi-only setting applies).

## Design and animations

- **Library in the manner of Apple Music** (with Ket 27): a large title, the categories in a list — Playlists, Artists, Albums, Titles, Favourites, Downloads, Local music, Replay — and *Recently added* as a grid of covers.
- **Pages that grow out of their cover.** An album or a playlist opened from its cover now grows out of it as a card whose corners straighten as it fills the screen, and folds back into it when closed.
- **Lists bounce at their ends** as on iPhone, everywhere in the app.
- **A heart that bursts** into small hearts when a title becomes a favourite in the player.
- **Living artwork.** The player's artwork pulses very slightly with the bass (Settings → Appearance).
- **A Liquid Glass app icon**: a "K" of clear glass capsules over a pink-to-blue ground (Settings → Appearance → App icon → Liquid Glass).
- **Ket 27 symbols**: the tab bar, the search button and the player's heart use new filled, rounded symbols in the manner of iOS.

## Privacy

- A search of three words or more is also sent to Genius to find titles by their lyrics. See PRIVACY.md.
