# Ketvoice 6.0.0 Beta 2

![Ketvoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tysnnn23/Ketvoice/main/ketvoice-banner.png)

The redesign of Ketvoice 6: one interface, built for phones and tablets, and the voice of a song in your hands.

## One interface

- **Ket 27 is now Ketvoice's interface**, everywhere. The classic interface and its switch are gone; every page, alert, button, progress bar and message follows the same design.
- **Lyrics are shown in Cascade**, the only lyrics style: the line being sung stands forward, the others follow it in a wave.
- **The glass menu is the menu of a song.** Press and hold a song, or touch its "…" button anywhere (lists, albums, playlists, Home, the player): the song lifts off the page and its menu unfolds from it. Every other option is one touch away under **More**.

## Tablets and large windows

- **A glass sidebar** with your tabs, library sections, pinned and recent playlists and search; it folds into a narrow rail of symbols, and medium windows get the rail directly. Phones keep their tab bar.
- **A mini-player docked at the bottom** of the page, which unfolds into the player.
- **Two panes** on album, playlist and artist pages (the artwork and details stay put while the songs scroll) and in search (your query and recent searches beside the results). Shelves show more whole cards.
- **A player made for large screens**: the lyrics beside the artwork in landscape, below it in portrait, the queue as a panel beside them when there is room; foldables keep everything clear of the hinge.
- **Sheets float** as centred panels and popovers instead of covering the screen.
- **Keyboard and mouse**: shortcuts as on a computer (Space to play or pause, arrows to seek and change the volume, Ctrl F to search, Ctrl L for the lyrics, Ctrl U for the queue, Esc to go back), secondary click on songs for their menu, hover on buttons, and drag a song onto a playlist of the sidebar.

## The voice of a song

- **Voice level**: on the lyrics screen, a glass slider beside the lyrics turns the singer down, from the full song to the instrumental alone, while it plays. Drag it, or touch the microphone to take the voice out and bring it back. It appears with the player's controls, not in full-screen lyrics.
- It works on downloaded songs once they are separated whole: while the phone charges, Ketvoice separates your downloads with the separation model (Settings → Ketmix).
- **Downloads show a music symbol** on the songs whose voice and music are separated.

## Ketmix

- Downloaded songs separated whole serve every mix they take part in, so their mixes are ready at once.

## Install

Download `Ketvoice-6.0.0-beta.2.apk` (phone) or `Ketvoice-6.0.0-beta.2-Quest.apk` (Meta Quest) below and install it over your current version: your library, settings and downloads stay. Settings for the classic interface and the lyrics style are simply no longer used.
